# Code of conduct

Backstage Forever is for players of every faction, level and background. In issues, pull requests and anywhere else the project lives:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Horde, Alliance and newcomers alike.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, and no sharing of others' private information.
- Don't post cheats, exploits against other players, or anything that breaks the game's rules.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block people who don't follow this. Problems can be reported privately the same way as security issues (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
