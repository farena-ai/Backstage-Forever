Backstage Forever is a free desktop app for World of Warcraft: Forever that reads your own saved characters and tells you what to do next: quests, talents, dungeons, professions and gold. It installs and updates its in-game addon for you, so the same guidance follows you into the game.

What Backstage Forever does

Everything below works from your own characters: the addon saves what it sees while you play, and the desktop app turns it into plans. Horde and Alliance are covered alike. Where the data is still thin, the data coverage page says so.

Inside WoW Forever

The addon brings it into the game

The Adventure Compass shows your experience, your pace, the quests that are ready to hand in and your next step. Tooltips show quest progress on enemies and whether an item is an upgrade for you.

You choose which hints each character gets, and every one can be switched off. Click the owl button on the minimap to ask the companion a question without leaving the game.
