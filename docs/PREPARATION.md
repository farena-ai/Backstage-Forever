# Preparation: recipes and shopping plans

Preparation uses what the companion addon could observe about the selected character. It does not need an AI connection. After loading the updated addon, use your profession normally if recipe information is missing; let WoW save on logout, or voluntarily reload when you want an immediate desktop snapshot. The desktop cannot force a game save.

Choose a known recipe and the number of craft batches. BF compares required reagents with the backpack and four bags when that observation is complete. A partial count is a lower bound; missing information is shown as unknown rather than zero. Output quantities may vary, so batches are not a promise of a particular number of finished items or skill points.

Add shopping checklist deliberately creates a local Company plan. Its steps are manual checks; BF does not craft, buy, move items, transfer from an alt or mark game progress from a shopping list. Other characters' dated observations are shown separately and are never silently pooled. Bank, account, guild and mail storage are outside this carried-material view.

Recipe lists can be partial or unavailable when the game's public APIs do not expose them safely. BF never opens profession windows, changes filters, or adopts another player's linked recipe list to fill a gap. Characters with unavailable observations can still use manual Company checklists. Existing notes and private goals remain private, and preparation refreshes do not send an AI request.

Later historical seller references, where available, describe previously observed merchants and approximate interaction locations. They are not a promise of current stock, price, access or faction eligibility. No observed seller means this reference has no matching observation, not that the material is unobtainable.
