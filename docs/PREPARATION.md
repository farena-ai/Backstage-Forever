# Preparation: recipes and shopping plans

Preparation uses what the companion addon could observe about the selected character. It does not need an AI connection. After loading the updated addon, use your profession normally if recipe information is missing; let WoW save on logout, or voluntarily reload when you want an immediate desktop snapshot. The desktop cannot force a game save.

Choose a known recipe and the number of craft batches. BF compares required reagents with the backpack and four bags when that observation is complete. A partial count is a lower bound; missing information is shown as unknown rather than zero. Output quantities may vary, so batches are not a promise of a particular number of finished items or skill points.

Add shopping checklist deliberately creates a local Company plan. Its steps are manual checks; BF does not craft, buy, move items, transfer from an alt or mark game progress from a shopping list. Other characters' dated observations are shown separately and are never silently pooled. Bank, account, guild and mail storage are outside this carried-material view.

Recipe lists can be partial or unavailable when the game's public APIs do not expose them safely. BF never opens profession windows, changes filters, or adopts another player's linked recipe list to fill a gap. Characters with unavailable observations can still use manual Company checklists. Existing notes and private goals remain private, and preparation refreshes do not send an AI request.

## Historical places to look

A separate local acquisition data increment is ready for a following profession/map consumer; no new preparation UI or native marker is connected by this increment. It holds seven material/source relations and seventeen approximate historical standing points: Silverleaf (765), Peacebloom (2447), Earthroot (2449), Copper Ore (2770), Rough Stone (2835), corpse-looted Linen Cloth (2589), and the historical Coarse Thread merchant listing (2320). These item labels describe the reference coverage; a consumer must display material names from actual same-ID recipe/material observations. Source labels are bounded reference names of unspecified locale.

All seven relations retain build `1.60.1.70862` and November 9, 2026 point observations. Matching dated addon evidence can qualify a historical place to look; a `70888` character, manual profile or undated snapshot can only browse this older reference. The merchant additionally needs wholly nonhostile reaction observations for the saved faction. This pin has Horde `[5]`, with Alliance access unknown. Corpse loot remains distinct from gathering; quest and `bountifulHarvest:1` contexts are retained without declaring them requirements. Gathering rank/skill, current presence, access, stock and availability stay unknown. The points are where a contributor stood, never exact spawns or a promise of gatherability. No observation does not mean unobtainable. Public 0.12.3 is unchanged and 1.0 remains held.

Historical seller references describe observed merchants and approximate interaction positions. Review the collection date, build and faction qualification. Qualified suggestions and browse-only references are different; missing Alliance reaction evidence stays unknown, and multiple-map mobile history is browse-only. Current stock, price and access remain unverified. No observed seller means the catalog has no matching observation, not that the material is unobtainable. BF does not generate vendor game-waypoint commands.

## Ask about the selected supply plan

**Ask about this supply plan** opens an editable AI question using this selected character's visible profession, recipe, batches, material counts, observation dates/scope and bounded historical seller qualifications. Partial counts stay partial; BF does not manufacture an exact shortfall. Other characters, private notes/goals/journeys, equipment and hidden identifiers are excluded.

Review/edit the question, then explicitly **Send question** if wanted. Opening the draft does not contact an AI provider or select saved conversation memory. Your existing **Use my active character** preference stays unchanged. Anything deliberately included in the visible question goes to the chosen provider when you send it, even when automatic character sharing is off. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).
