# Professions: one workshop for recipes and shopping plans

Preparation uses what the companion addon could observe about the selected character. It does not need an AI connection. After loading the updated addon, use your profession normally if recipe information is missing; let WoW save on logout, or voluntarily reload when you want an immediate desktop snapshot. The desktop cannot force a game save.

Open **Adventure → Professions** (or the shortcut in Plans). Auto chooses among observed owned learned skill-up recipes, preferring established carried supply, then fewer missing material types and units. A partial recipe catalog is sufficient for known entries; this is not a cheapest/fastest leveling guide. You may choose a profession, an explicit saved recipe, and 1–20 craft attempts. These choices autosave for this character and remain independent of the native Compass goals. An explicitly unavailable recipe is never silently switched. Recipes that cannot currently skill up remain clearly labeled crafting plans. BF compares required reagents with the backpack and four bags when that observation is complete. A partial count is a lower bound; missing information is shown as unknown rather than zero. Output quantities may vary, so batches are not a promise of a particular number of finished items or skill points.

Add shopping checklist deliberately creates a local plan in **Plans → My plans**. Its steps are manual checks; BF does not craft, buy, move items, transfer from an alt or mark game progress from a shopping list. Other characters' dated observations are shown separately and are never silently pooled. Bank, account, guild and mail storage are outside this carried-material view.

Recipe lists can be partial or unavailable when the game's public APIs do not expose them safely. BF never opens profession windows, changes filters, or adopts another player's linked recipe list to fill a gap. Characters with unavailable observations can still use manual checklists in **Plans → My plans**. Existing notes and private goals remain private, and preparation refreshes do not send an AI request.

Gathering rank/skill, current presence, access, stock and availability stay unknown. The points are where a contributor stood, never exact spawns or a promise of gatherability. No observation does not mean unobtainable. Public 0.12.3 is unchanged and 1.0 remains held.

Historical seller references describe observed merchants and approximate interaction positions. Review the collection date, build and faction qualification. Qualified suggestions and browse-only references are different; missing Alliance reaction evidence stays unknown, and multiple-map mobile history is browse-only. Current stock, price and access remain unverified. No observed seller means the catalog has no matching observation, not that the material is unobtainable. BF does not generate vendor game-waypoint commands.

## Ask about the selected supply plan

**Ask about this supply plan** opens an editable AI question using this selected character's visible profession, recipe, batches, material counts, observation dates/scope and bounded historical seller/acquisition qualifications and observed contexts. Partial counts stay partial; BF does not manufacture an exact shortfall. Other characters, private notes/goals/journeys, equipment and hidden identifiers are excluded.

Review/edit the question, then explicitly **Send question** if wanted. Opening the draft does not contact an AI provider or select saved conversation memory. Your existing **Use my active character** preference stays unchanged. Anything deliberately included in the visible question goes to the chosen provider when you send it, even when automatic character sharing is off. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).


Observed rank progress is shown as rank / trained cap, not predicted skill gain. At the cap, check training before expecting progress. Gathering professions with a supported observed method can browse the relevant historical resource observations even when no learned crafting recipe is present. Requirements, current nodes and travel paths remain unknown. Historical vendor coverage also includes Strange Dust (10940) and Lesser Magic Essence (10938); those listings do not establish disenchant sources, and essence is not a shard.

The addon setup now owns eight files: TOC, Reference, Plan, Coach, Acquisition, Profession, capture and HUD. Setup reads them back, repairs missing/outdated owned files and preserves foreign files. Activate the new files once at game launch or voluntary reload; normal in-game updates do not require repeated reloads. This is local source/automated validation at 0.12.3; live client acceptance and 1.0 remain held.
