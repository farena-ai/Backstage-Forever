# Party plans: prepare together, share deliberately

Open **Plans → Party** to create a manual dungeon-preparation checklist and exchange a small plan file. It does not log into a party service, send chat messages, control group members or operate the game.

Choose a role yourself: tank, healer, damage, or unspecified. BF may show the selected character's observed class, level, equipment and bag space with their saved date. These are facts to review, not a gear score or guarantee of dungeon access. Repair, consumables and meeting-point checks remain manual unless an actual observation explicitly says otherwise.

Create a dungeon checklist to add a local journey in **Plans → My plans**. Check or edit its steps as your group prepares. Viewing Party does not change your selected character or re-enable following.

To share an existing plan, select it and open the preview. The portable title starts generically. Review every line and remove personal wording before explicitly saving the JSON file. Only the disclosed title and step text are portable; BF does not automatically append profiles, journal notes, goals, provider credentials, source identifiers or completion evidence. Text you deliberately wrote into the selected steps may itself contain personal information, so the preview matters.

Share the saved file yourself using your preferred method. BF does not message anyone or upload it to a sharing service.

To receive a plan, choose Import plan and inspect its preview. Opening the file alone does not add anything. Explicitly add it to Company when satisfied. The resulting steps receive new local IDs, start unchecked and unassigned, and carry no imported quest proof. A portable plan never replaces your character roster or backup. Imported text is inert text, not an instruction BF executes.

If Company is at its plan limit, remove an unwanted local plan deliberately before importing another. Failed or cancelled imports leave the current journal intact. Keep profile backup/restore separate from party-plan exchange.

## Guild Run Pack

In **Plans → Party → Guild Run Pack**, choose **Create run** to write a dated event sheet. Enter the organizer's date and time, a named time zone (such as `America/New_York`), and the UTC offset on that date (such as `-05:00`). The offset disambiguates repeated times when clocks go back. A time skipped when clocks go forward cannot be scheduled. Add role duties and concrete prep actions as separate lines.

**Preview run** shows the exact shareable plain text. Your device's local time is shown separately and is not exported; the shared copy retains the organizer's time zone and UTC start. Review the wording for anything personal, then **Save run locally**. **Export JSON** makes a copy another member can import; **Export plain text** makes a copy for someone without the app. You send the file yourself. To change or cancel a run, reopen it under **Saved runs → Organized runs** and review the next revision. A cancellation is a new copy, not a way to retract old copies, and carries no duties or prep actions.

Members choose **Import run pack** to review a JSON file, including the start in their own local time. Nothing is added until they confirm. Duplicate, older, and conflicting revisions do not replace the saved run. A newer revision preserves checks only for unchanged prep actions. A cancellation retires the checklist. Checks are private to this installation and are never included in exports.

Guild Run Packs are separate from ordinary Party plans, Company journeys, and journal backups. This first version keeps up to 32 organized runs and 32 received runs locally per installation. It does not use an account, relay, RSVP collection, or live sync.

### Officer debrief and next-run handoff

Open **Saved runs → Organized runs**, select the run, then choose **Open officer debrief**. Record confirmed attendance, progression, assignments and follow-ups yourself. A Yes RSVP, a board assignment or a game observation never fills in attendance. Leave unknown facts blank and keep anything you do not want to share in **Private officer note**.

Choose **Save confirmed debrief**, then **Review next-run copy**. The preview shows the run title and the saved facts you entered in the shareable fields. Read every line before choosing **Copy reviewed text**: the app does not redact personal wording you type into those fields. The private officer note, organizer board records, private after-action review, loot records and journal are never pulled into the copy. Paste the text yourself where you want it to go. Copying does not create or reschedule a run, change a Run Pack export, or send a message.

Debriefs stay on this installation and follow the organized run across revisions. A cancelled run's debrief is read-only and cannot be copied. Received runs do not have access to the organizer's debrief, even when the same installation imports a copy. Resetting local data removes the local debriefs, but cannot recall text you have already copied or shared.
