# Original artwork and typography

The 0.2.0 interface uses original raster illustrations created with built-in image generation and bundled locally. No Blizzard artwork, logos, portraits or extracted game assets were copied. World of Warcraft was an aesthetic reference for the requested rich MMORPG feel; these scenes and emblems are independent designs. Branding and product design: Frank Arena / Backstage Coding.

- `assets/citadel-journey.png`: painted mountain citadel, ancient forest, lanterns, magical light, a ranger and a silver wolf; dark left space keeps real interface text legible.
- `assets/wolf-medallion.png`: silver wolf portrait in an engraved bronze ring with teal gems and dark leather. Decorative companion emblem, not a live player portrait.
- `assets/crest.svg`: original Backstage identity. Earlier original vector landscape remains unused in assets/history.

## Prompt set

Citadel production prompt: spectacular original high-fantasy MMORPG environment; production background, no mockup/UI/text/logos/watermarks; ultrawide 21:9. Rich hand-painted premium loading-screen style, chunky heroic architecture, exquisite atmospheric depth and tactile brushwork, epic scale and nostalgia, comparable ambition and fantasy feel to WoW with completely original setting and characters. Ancient weathered blue-gray mountain citadel, crooked towers and amber windows above mist valley, snowy peaks, huge gnarled evergreens, bronze lanterns, teal runes and wisps, golden sunrise through storm, distant magical blue beam. Far-right leather/bronze hooded ranger with bow and large silver wolf, back view, crimson cloth, gold armor, no face. Diagonal journey path, volumetric light, enchanted green/midnight blue/warm orange/aged gold. Left third dark and spacious for ivory title overlay. Full-bleed detailed painting, no border, credible materials, no flat vector or geometric mountains, no corporate illustration or photography. High resolution, crisp detail and cinematic luminosity.

Medallion production prompt: original premium high-fantasy MMORPG square medallion for Backstage Forever; no text/letters/watermarks/copied icon. Sculpted silver wolf in three-quarter frontal view, amber eyes and textured fur, noble heroic expression. Thick ornate aged-bronze ring, hammered/engraved interlacing, small teal gems, warm firelight and cyan rimlight. Dark forest-green/black leather inset, embossed detail. Ring nearly fills square with margins; near-black leather surround allows CSS circular masking. High-end hand-painted game-item finish, dimensional bevel, material highlights/shadows, restrained magic and readable small silhouette. No flat vector/corporate illustration. Original fantasy craftsmanship.

## Fonts

Cinzel and Source Sans 3 are bundled from the Google Fonts repository. SIL Open Font License notices are included under assets/fonts. No remote font service is contacted.
