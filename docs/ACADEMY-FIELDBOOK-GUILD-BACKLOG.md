# Backstage Forever — Academy, Fieldbook, and Guild Operations

This is the detailed backlog for the post-1.12 [delivery order](FOREVER-COMPANION-NEXT-ROADMAP.md#post-112-delivery-order). It extends the [existing backlog](FOREVER-EXPANSION-BACKLOG.md), not its scope or release promises. **Next** means the next cut in that wave; **Later** means after its first complete workflow; **Gated** means a live-client, evidence, consent, or deployment dependency. All entries below are planned, not shipped.

## Wave 3 — Dungeon Fieldbook and first guild workflow

| ID | Priority | Build on → deliver | Acceptance witness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BF-F01 | Next | Instance Atlas, saved quests, gear wishlist and Journal → one complete first-run Fieldbook: entrance, quest pickups, supplies, role prep, reviewed encounter cards, exact wishlist links and personal recap. | Verify one complete run workflow; missing observation is not a clear, personal drop or lockout. Keep reference material visibly separate from verified Forever facts. |
| BF-G01 | Next, after BF-F01 | [Plans → Party](PARTY-PLANS.md) and [Company](ADVENTURE-COMPANY.md) → **Guild Run Pack**: leader creates a reusable event sheet and explicitly exports a previewed copy. Members import it or read a plain-text copy, get local-time start time, role duties, exact prep actions, and a private checklist. | A leader and member can exchange a plan without an account or hosted service. Time zone/DST, revised/cancelled plans, duplicate import and redaction are tested. A copied file is not live sync; support optional manual RSVP replies and local status entry, not background collection. |
| BF-G02 | Later | Company → local officer run board: manually entered yes/maybe/no, tentative role coverage, bench and backup slots, recurring event and team templates, assignments and handoff notes. Keep roster edits and event-specific sign-ups distinct. | Role selection, coverage gaps, late changes and substitute moves are editable with visible history. A sign-up is not attendance; moving a person is never an in-game invite, promotion, or action. |
| BF-G03 | Later | Fieldbook and Journey → a member event inbox, own prep/reminders/calendar export, casual dungeon or quest groups, newcomer/mentor guides, and leader debrief with manually confirmed attendance, progression, follow-ups and next-run copy. | Local reminders are opt-in; revised or cancelled events do not keep sending stale alerts. Private notes and participant loot do not leak into shared recaps. |
| BF-G04 | Later | Workshop and goals → voluntary crafting directory, guild supply requests, donated/reserved/available quantities, and guild goal boards for raids, crafting or social nights. | Players choose what to advertise. A request or pledge is not inventory, and no cash, items, mails or bank records are moved automatically. |
| BF-G05 | Later | Officer board → append-only manual loot decisions, attendance corrections, officer handoff, recruiting needs and a shareable welcome pack. Keep recruitment publication manual. | Authorized corrections keep their history; loot is never awarded or implied from a boss kill, members are not ranked by hidden telemetry, and officer notes are never included in a public export. |
| BF-G06 | Gated | Run Pack → optional private two-client sharing for RSVPs/event revisions, scoped member/officer/raid-lead permissions, revocable invitations and channel-specific notification preferences. | Real distinct clients prove role changes, revocation, edits and conflicts, guild separation and offline fallback. Do not assume native roster, bank or calendar access; test read-only documented hooks and guild permissions on the exact build before any integration. No hosting or deploy without separate approval. |
| BF-G07 | Gated, after BF-G06 | Evaluate calendar/Discord integration beyond manual copy or `.ics` export, and read-only guild-bank views where supported, only if users need them. | Official API/terms, minimal permissions, opt-in preview, disconnect/revoke and rollback are proven. No chat scraping, automated advertising, guild-account credentials, or auto-posting by default. |

**Guild privacy default:** Members choose what they share. No default collection of guild chat, roster, alts, inventories, performance or private journals; no member/officer notes or plans in provider context. Scope authorized sharing by guild, role and event, use minimal fields, and make access and export contents reviewable. Revocation can stop future access to synced data, not retract already exported files; say so in the preview. Private officer-only notes stay separate even when guild membership is visible in game.
