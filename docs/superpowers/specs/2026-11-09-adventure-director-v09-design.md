Consume the existing validated saved profile and buildGuidance result rather than constructing a second quest database. Produce a bounded pure Director model: character public identity, saved freshness, up to three ranked quest choices, optional low-bag-space preparation fact, up to three private pending goals and honest limitations. The bag fact keeps 0.8's native threshold: positive total and zero through two free slots; missing/zero capacity stays quiet. A primary choice and two alternatives explain their source and reason. Unknown race/faction/location stays unknown; character identity is context, not an invented eligibility rule.

Ranking: ready turn-ins first, then current-game same-map destinations/observed same-zone objectives, then compatible approximate same-map reference positions, then reported quest-level proximity, then numeric ID. Distances are same-map straight-line comparisons only and cannot become guaranteed travel times/routes. Current game source/phase wins; reference-only mentioned NPCs remain clues. Keep existing detailed guidance and focus/minute options. No cloud call occurs when plans refresh. Rank before limiting: expose `guidance.candidates`, bounded to all 25 eligible observed quests through the same existing step-enrichment path, while retaining the existing visible `steps` slice and counts. Director consumes candidates, then caps its choices at three. This prevents a useful later quest being lost before ranking. Existing Explain drafting continues to whitelist visible public step facts, never private Director goals.
