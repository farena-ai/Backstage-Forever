## Planner contract

Focused src/core/travel-data.cjs validates/compiles/loads; src/core/travel.cjs handles intent/selection/displayable results. Contract exports documented by implementer; following names preferred:
- loadTravelReference(bytes) validated local pack.
- parseTravelIntent(question,{previousQuestion,profile}) returns null for nontravel, or {status:'ready'|'clarify',originId,destinationId,question,clarification?,originSource:'explicit'|'saved-location'|'unknown'}. Lexical IDs from fixed aliases, never execute/interpret arbitrary data. At most12000 chars. org, tb, uc, sw, if only recognized standalone in a travel question, never URL/substring aliases. Treat the conjunction if as ordinary grammar; accept IF for Ironforge only in an unambiguous explicit endpoint position, or omit that abbreviation and use Ironforge. Leveling/questing questions such as how do I get to level20 and where should I quest next are not transport intent. Ambiguous targets/multiple trips/there with no trustworthy original intent ask one clear question. Explicit origin wins over saved; known last-saved profile.location may supply current origin with qualification. shareProfile:false means NO saved location input. No private notes/goals/source IDs/paths, active quest destination guessing, or AI-text-derived intent. Correction only e.g 'Forever not retail' may reuse previous original USER question from approved conversation service, never model reply. Unrelated new question must not inherit stale trip.
- planTravel(reference,intent,{profile}) returns {status:'route'|'clarify'|'uncovered'|'same-place'|'unavailable',text,origin,destination,options:[{id,title,mode,conditional,steps:[plain text],sourceIds}],cautions:[plain text],sources:[qualified metadata],coverage}. Unknown faction does not assume travel eligibility; opposing faction does not offer faction-restricted transport as usable. Exactbuild flight requires matching observed addon build; manual/mismatched/unknown build may receive qualified general guide fallback but cannot promote community exact-build flight as current proof. Published general guides stay general and date-labelled. No raw coordinates masquerading as travel or fabricated waypoint commands. Sameplace means named city only, not precise arrival.

OrgTB example for matching Horde observed build: lead with 'Check Doras for Thunder Bluff. If it is offered, take that flight; your unlocked routes are unknown.' Provide separately titled documented road fallback. Explain no direct zeppelin in published Forever guide; other routes exist. 'Fastest' answer says available flight may save walking, actual fastest unknown; no confident global speed. If pack invalid/unavailable fail closed to honest local unavailable reply for recognized travel rather than ungrounded provider guess. Include sources/conditions even in plain text result so legacy ask later cannot lose them.
