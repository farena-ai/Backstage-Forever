Automatic recipe policy: among safely observed owned learned recipes in the chosen profession, consider explicitly canSkillUp=true entries whose recipe observation is no older than profession rank observation and whose unambiguous positive reagent schematic is known. The whole recipe catalog can be partial; do not discard valid known entries. Prefer enough known carried supply, then fewest distinct missing materials, then fewest missing units, then stable recipe ID. Describe this as using observed supplies, never cheapest/fastest/optimal or guaranteed rank gain. Unknown quantities cannot win over established availability. If no skillLineId is selected, choose a profession with a useful observed eligible recipe first; otherwise the first stable owned profession. Never silently replace an explicit missing/mismatched recipe selection: explain unavailable and offer Auto/another recipe. Manual selection of an observed learned recipe that cannot currently skill up remains a crafting plan, clearly not a leveling recommendation.

Partial counts are lower bounds; absent items in partial observations are unknown, not zero. Reuse existing requirements cap100000 per reagent, at most10 per recipe, attempts1–20; sums checked finite/safe in both JS and Lua. Batches are attempts, not guaranteed output/skill points or an automatically decremented count of performed crafts. Observed trained-cap rank==maxRank gets an explicit check-training qualification before expecting progress; no promised higher cap, unlock, training cost, NPC offer or skill-up chance.

Gathering professions without a learned crafting recipe still show their observed rank and relevant historical resource references when their method is supported. Seven acquisition relations are modest coverage, not a complete profession database. No source match means not covered, not unobtainable. requiredRank/requiredSkillLineId/availableNow remain unknown. Historic loot/interaction points are approximate player-standing positions, not nodes/exact NPC coordinates. Keep corpse quest-context separate from skinning and preserve bountifulHarvest context.
