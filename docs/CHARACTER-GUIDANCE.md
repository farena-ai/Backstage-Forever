# Character guidance

Open **Characters** to see a local field guide for the active saved character. Adventure's **Character guidance** action opens the same page. There are no extra role or setup questions, and no AI connection is needed. Supported game saves refresh this view automatically; the desktop still reflects the last saved observation, never the live game.

The header preserves the exact saved name, class, race, faction, level and realm. A custom race such as **Sunborn** remains Sunborn. Source kind, saved date, client build and locale appear beside those facts. Manual journals, absent metadata, incompatible build/locale, future dates and observations older than seven days receive distinct labels. A stale observation is dated context, not a current readiness check. Missing supplementary reference data disables only the historical enrichment.

## Three kinds of evidence

- **Saved character facts:** exact identity and up to 19 observed equipment rows. Each row shows its item ID, saved name, numeric quality and reported item level where available. Missing values stay unknown. Item level ascending, unknown last, is a convenient review order, not an upgrade or suitability score. No compulsory missing-slot list is generated.
- **Matching-build community observations:** the separate Efficient Games CC BY 4.0 supplementary reference is pinned to build `1.60.1.70862` and English reference text. Observation-name locale is unspecified. It contains 34 trainers, 2,074 service listings and 1,139 distinct spell IDs; those are historical listings, not a complete spellbook. Names alone provide no stats or equip permissions. If a saved item name differs from its exact-ID reference name, both remain separately labelled.
- **Dated general class identity:** nine tiny summaries describe broad class themes from Blizzard's [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304075/create-the-hero-you-want-to-be-in-world-of-warcraft-forever), checked November 10, 2026. This is a general overview, not verified mechanics for the saved client build. Unknown classes receive no invented summary. The official page does not establish racial bonuses, including Sunborn traits.

Readable equipment labels are independently written ordinary English labels for inventory constants in the matching UI source: [Gethe `Constants.lua`, immutable pin `9dc84f0d3bbae1768ac3405b3716000bd93eda06`](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/9dc84f0d3bbae1768ac3405b3716000bd93eda06/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_FrameXMLBase/Constants.lua#L134). SHA256: `464da9fb28ed086d09f9f7c47a54888c7d47b039415d8475ac825f51f8506290`. Slots 1–19 are Head, Neck, Shoulder, Body, Chest, Waist, Legs, Feet, Wrist, Hands, Ring 1, Ring 2, Trinket 1, Trinket 2, Back, Main hand, Off hand, Ranged and Tabard. Other builds use numeric labels. These labels do not prove equip permissions or mandatory slots; Body, Tabard, off hand and ranged are not universally required. Numeric quality is displayed without assuming a quality-name mapping.

## Historical training review

A personal historical review shows at most three trainers and six listings per trainer. It requires an addon source, exact reference build, `enUS` saved locale, an exact English `Class Trainer` title and only nonhostile historical reactions for the saved faction. Type zero alone does not identify a taught class. Missing Alliance/other faction evidence means access is unknown, never inaccessible.

All services are sorted before limiting the displayed list. Positive reported levels nearest the saved character level come first; zero is unknown and comes last. This is review ordering, never proof that a spell is learned, unlearned or eligible. Current location, access, prerequisites and costs remain unverified. Positions are approximate historical observations, with no trainer waypoint action.

The separate **Browse historical training reference** disclosure searches trainer names, class titles and listed spell names, including listings without personal faction evidence. Search accepts at most 120 characters and shows at most eight trainers with six listings each. Browse results never become personal access suggestions. Source details state dates, build, licence, separate source-file counts and omitted positions. Extra facts update with app releases; the existing quest-cache refresh is separate.

## Manual plans and optional questions

**Create character checklist** makes one local Company journey with at most ten manual checks: review current equipment, up to six displayed equipment rows, supported historical class training, unverified racial traits, and talents/known spells. Every step begins unchecked and has no observed quest proof. BF performs no game action. Existing Company limits still apply: at most 20 journeys and 50 steps per journey. The entire new plan is validated before a single save; capacity or write failure cannot leave partial steps.

Repeated use of the same displayed choice opens its existing plan and preserves subsequent edits/completion. After navigation, app restart or replacement, a plan with the same title, ordered manual text and profile assignments is reused regardless of its checkmarks. An edited or renamed plan is never overwritten; a new deliberately requested plan may be created when no identical plan remains. Deleted plans can be recreated. Fresh source/profile/reference validation rejects held stale choices. Confirmed import, reset and recovery restore explicitly retire pending choices and reads, even when restored public facts are identical. Ordinary private-note saves do not invalidate unchanged public guidance.

**Ask about this character** opens a bounded, editable public question in AI Companion. It never sends, selects memory or changes **Use my active character**. Review the draft before sending; the deliberately selected public facts in the draft are still part of the question even when automatic character sharing is off. Private notes, goals, journeys, memory, provider/model settings and character/source identifiers or choice tokens are excluded. No added facts enter automatic AI context, Director ranking, addon capture or native guidance.

Racial traits, talents, known spells, equip permissions, item stats, DPS and best-in-slot advice remain unverified. Check those in the game. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md), [Company](ADVENTURE-COMPANY.md) and [Forever data](FOREVER-DATA.md).
