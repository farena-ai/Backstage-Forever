# Your Adventure Director

Open **Adventure** and choose your character. Director gives you one explained next quest action and up to two alternatives from that character's captured quests. You do not need an AI connection.

Ready turn-ins come first. Other choices consider the reported map/zone, compatible approximate reference positions and reported quest level. Your race/class/faction are shown as character context; missing data does not become a guessed quest restriction or route. An NPC mentioned in an objective is a clue, not automatically its finisher.

## Make a session in one click

Choose the existing session length and quest focus, then **Start quest session**. BF creates a tracked Company journey from the currently saved character's selected quests and opens it. The 15/30/60-minute choices track at most one/two/three quests. They limit the plan's size; they do not promise completion times.

If a journey already tracks that exact selected quest set from the same saved baseline, BF reuses it even if you renamed it or added manual notes. More or different tracked quests make it a different plan. Starting a session does not send an AI question or perform a game action. Manual characters can keep using Company checklists; they cannot manufacture observed game evidence.

Tracking starts from the chosen save. A newer compatible save may prove a new turn-in/completion, with observed and manual checks labelled separately. A ready quest or one missing from the log is not automatically complete. [Company](ADVENTURE-COMPANY.md) explains repeatables, missing history and retained evidence.

## Facts, goals and freshness

Director can show a quiet preparation fact when the saved bags have two or fewer free slots in a positive capacity. Your pending goals are local prompts chosen by you. They are not automatically copied into the quest session or sent to AI. Existing **Explain my plan** opens a bounded editable public quest-fact question; nothing is sent until **Send question**.

The desktop uses the last game save. Natural logout or exit updates it; a voluntary reload is available when you specifically want an immediate save. The in-game Compass uses supported current observations outside combat and continues with BF closed. Routine objective/level changes do not require repeated reloads.

The planner considers the bounded captured quest slice before selecting its top cards. It does not know every quest, travel unlock, spawn, prerequisite, dungeon requirement or complete account history. Same-map coordinates do not establish a safe path or travel time. Unknown facts stay unknown.
