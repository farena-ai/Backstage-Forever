# Backstage Forever 0.34.0 — the community map

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Players now help each other find things.** When you gather an herb, mine ore, open a chest or meet an NPC, the app can share *where* it was, so everyone's World Map fills in faster.

- **What is shared:** the thing found (herb, ore, chest or NPC id), the zone, and a position rounded to a coarse grid. Nothing else: no character names, no realm, no account, no chat and no IP address is kept. Your install has a random id, and the server only stores a hash of it.
- **What you get back:** once a day, one small download of finds that **at least three different players agree on**. They show on the World Map, on NPC cards and in the loot browser, marked as community finds. Everything you found yourself still works offline, as before.
- **Your choice:** sharing is on by default. Turn it off, or erase what you sent, in **Settings → Community**.
- **Report a problem:** Settings → Community → Report a problem shows you **exactly** what will be sent before you send it.

The service runs on Backstage Coding's own server at bf.backstagecoding.com, with no paid services. Until it's reachable, the app simply waits and tries again later; nothing else changes.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once.
