# Backstage Forever 1.0

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**Backstage Forever is 1.0.** It's a free leveling companion for World of Warcraft: Forever. A desktop app and an in-game addon read your own game, tell you what to do next and guide you there. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing plays for you.

**In the game**
- **Adventure Compass:** XP pace, quests ready to hand in and your next step, in a small panel that hides in combat.
- **Guided route** for your race (`/bf route guide`), and arrows to your next quest, trainer or flight master (`/bf next`, `/bf train`, `/bf find flight`).
- **Quest progress in enemy tooltips**, auto-dismount at flight masters, a Sell junk button and quiet enemy-player alerts.
- **Ask panel:** click the owl on the minimap and ask in plain words.

**On the desktop**
- **Now:** what to do next, live while you play, with Mini Now beside the game.
- **World Map** with your position, quests, trainers, flight paths and gathering spots.
- **Companion:** ask anything in everyday words, such as "wheres the apothecary dude in xroads", "fastest way to tb from xroads" or "who drops the huge gnoll claw". It answers from WoW Forever's own data and says when it doesn't know. With your own AI model it answers in character, still from those facts.
- **Professions** advice from your real recipes, **Dungeons** with a painting for each of the 27, **Characters** with your own portrait (race, sex and class), gear sheet, talents, ledger and journal.
- German and English. Horde and Alliance alike.

**Install:** Windows: `Backstage-Forever-1.0.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe`. Mac: the macOS zip for your chip. The app installs and updates the addon for you. For the addon alone: `BackstageForever-Addon-1.0.0.zip` into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.

**Updating from a preview:** the app updates itself to 1.0. Then type `/reload` in WoW once.

**Known limits:** party sync needs a second player with the addon; the Mac app has been checked on GitHub's Macs, not yet by a person on a real Mac; German in-game messages need a German game client.
