# Backstage Forever 0.40.0 — enemy player alerts

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Know who's around.** When an enemy player who can attack you becomes your target, passes under your mouse or shows a nameplate, Backstage Forever says so at the top of your screen: "Alliance level 32 Rogue nearby", or "Horde level ?? Warrior" when they're far above you. Chat gets their name and the zone. It works the same for Horde and Alliance players.

It stays quiet when it should. Each player alerts once and again only after five minutes out of sight. A group of five gives one alert, not five. Friendly players, players who can't attack you and battlegrounds or arenas never alert.

**`/bf enemy`** lists the last players you saw: name, faction, level, class, zone and how long ago. `/bf enemy sound on` adds a warning sound, `/bf enemy off` turns the alerts off and `/bf enemy clear` empties the list.

The addon only reads what the game already shows you. It never targets, marks or reports anyone, and it doesn't tell other players.

**After updating:** quit WoW and start it again once, because this version adds a new addon file. `/reload` isn't enough.
