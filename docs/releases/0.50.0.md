# Backstage Forever 0.50.0 — guided route

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Follow one step at a time, from level 1.** In WoW, type `/bf route guide`. The scroll on your screen shows the next step on your race's route: pick up these quests, do this objective, hand in to this person, head to the next zone. The game's arrow points at the step, and the guide moves on by itself as you pick up and hand in quests.

- `/bf route skip` skips the step you're on.
- If you're well ahead of a step's level, the guide moves past it. If you're behind, it tells you.
- Class quests show up as one step: "take the quest for your class, if they have one."
- `/bf route zone` goes back to the quests around you in this zone. `/bf route` hides the scroll.

**In the app: Adventure → Guided route.** Your route, how far along you are, the next steps with a Map button for each, and a table of how far every race's route reaches.

**What's covered today.** The routes are built from the quest data we have, and they're honest about the gaps:

- Human, Orc, Night Elf, Undead and Troll: levels 1 to about 42.
- Tauren: 1 to 42, except levels 4 to 9.
- Dwarf and Gnome: 10 to 42. We have no Dun Morogh quests yet.

Past the end of a route, the zone guide and Now's "Where to level" take over. The routes grow as community data and your own play fill the gaps. Next comes the hand review: going through each route step by step, approving, skipping and reordering.

**After updating:** quit WoW and start it again once, because this version adds a new addon file.
