# Backstage Forever 0.25.0 — always current

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Done means done.** With the live link on, the addon now also sends your quest log, and the quests you hand in, while you play. A quest you just turned in drops off Now right away and shows under **Just completed** with a green Completed tag. Quests you pick up appear in the log, and finished ones move to "Hand in", all without a `/reload`. The Quest log card says whether it's **Live** or from your last save.

**A tidier Now page.**
- The cards sit in two independent columns, so there's no more empty space between boxes.
- The seven main links on the left fit at the app's normal size, with no scroll bar.
- Your status line shows how old your last save is.

**Reload banner with times.** If you `/reload` a few seconds before an update finishes copying the addon, the banner now gives both times ("27 seconds before the update landed") so you know one more `/reload` fixes it.

**The companion works with no AI set up.** Ask "how do I do Raptor Thieves?", "what should I do next?" or "what do I train?" and get an answer instantly, from the app's own data. No model, account or internet needed. NPC and travel questions already worked this way.

**One click to get set up.** The Get set up card on Now has a **Set it all up** button. It finds WoW Forever, installs the addon and turns on the live link.

**Fixed:**
- Now could fail to load for a character with no saved location.
- Automated test windows no longer take keyboard focus while you play.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once so the game loads the new addon (it adds a second row to the small live strip at the top-left).
