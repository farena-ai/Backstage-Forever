# Backstage Forever 0.36.0 — your character

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**A character sheet in the app** (Characters → Sheet):
- Your equipped gear slot by slot, in quality colours, with item cards on hover and your average item level.
- Empty slots, pieces that have fallen behind your level, and upgrades you already own in your bags or bank.
- Your base stats, armor, attack power, health and mana, and resistances.

**Gear sets in game:** `/bf set save NAME`, `/bf set wear NAME`, `/bf set list` and `/bf set delete NAME`. Wearing a set equips the pieces from your bags only when you type it, and never in combat; anything not in your bags is named in chat. The Sheet lists each set and where every piece is: worn, in your bags, bank or mail, or on another character.

**A gold goal:** set your own target and a name for it on Ledger → Gold & prices. It shows progress across all your characters and, after a few days of play, about how many days are left at your pace.

Class quest chains and suggested mount costs are not included yet: the game data we use doesn't say which quests belong to which class, or what riding costs, and we won't guess.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once.
