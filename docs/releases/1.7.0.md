# Backstage Forever 1.7.0 — The painted field journal

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

- **A new look from the first launch.** The welcome art and desktop page banners have been repainted, with compositions tuned for wide and compact windows.
- **Artwork where it was missing.** The Ledger has distinct art for supplies, gold, reputation and cooldowns. The World Map title plaque, Settings and Field Guide have their own illustrations. Quest details and quest, NPC and item hover cards now have artwork that leaves the actual objectives, rewards and item icons easy to read.
- **At a glance, in and out of game.** Mini Now illustrates actionable steps, Ask has an illustrated welcome state, and the in-game Gear Sets menu has a painted footer without taking more room.
- **A more cohesive world.** Durotar, the Barrens and Elwynn Forest have matching artwork on desktop and in game. Ragefire Chasm and its final-boss reference have new paintings, and selected in-game notifications have freshly painted backgrounds.

Backstage Forever uses original illustrations. Actual map geometry, item information, gameplay behavior and your journal are unchanged.

Windows installs update automatically when updates are enabled. On macOS, download the matching zip and replace the app in Applications. Players using only the addon can install the standalone addon zip.
