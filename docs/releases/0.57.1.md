# Backstage Forever 0.57.1 — Professions read your recipes

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Professions work in WoW Forever.** Professions kept saying "Open a profession in game once" even after you'd opened it many times: no recipe was ever saved. The addon now reads the same recipe list the Professions window shows. Open each profession once and close it again. Your recipes, their skill-up colours and the next recipe worth making then appear in the app and in game. They stay after you close the window, `/reload` or log in again.

**Each profession once.** Tailoring, Cooking and the others no longer show up twice.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW, then open each of your professions once.
