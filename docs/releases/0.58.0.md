# Backstage Forever 0.58.0 — A companion that understands, and your character's own portrait

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**The companion understands you.** Ask the way you'd ask a friend: "wheres voljin at", "where's the gryphon guy in westfall", "who drops sharptalon's claw", "need food near sentinel hill", "take me to Booty Bay". The companion works out what you mean and answers from WoW Forever's own data: who and where someone is (with town and zone), which trainer to visit for your faction, who sells what, what drops what, where a quest turns in, what a dungeon holds, and how to catch the zeppelin, boat or tram. When it doesn't know, it says so instead of guessing.

**Measured, not guessed.** A new benchmark of 100 real player questions, checked against the game data, went from 15% answered well to 97%.

**In character.** Write in character ("Lok'tar ogar!", "*bows*") and the companion answers in character, true to your race, faction and class, while keeping the facts straight. It now firmly declines gold duping, hacks, AFK bots and getting into someone else's account.

**Your character, painted.** Every character picture now matches your race, sex and class. A female Forsaken priest gets a female Forsaken priest. There are 112 original portraits, including both Skyborne races. Characters shows yours as a framed portrait.

**Dungeons and raids.** Each of the 27 has its own painting at the top of its card.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once so the addon records your character's sex for the portrait.
