# Backstage Forever 0.35.0 — the ledger

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Fixed:** the "BackstageForever has been blocked from an action only available to the Blizzard UI" warning. The game only lets addons place its waypoint arrow when you click or type a command, and Backstage Forever was also placing it on its own (the BF Route window at login, and the arrow to your body after you released your spirit). Now the arrow moves only when you ask: `/bf route`, the Route window's < and > buttons, a map pin, or commands like `/bf next`, `/bf train` and `/bf way`. After a death, the game's own marker shows your body on the minimap and map.

**A new Ledger section** keeps track of everything your characters own, so you no longer log in to each one to check:

- **Bags & bank:** search every character's bags, bank and mailbox at once. Bank and mail show as last seen, with how long ago that was. Open the bank or mailbox once per character to fill them in.
- **Gold & prices:** gold per character and in total. When you browse the Auction House yourself, the addon remembers the lowest price per item it saw. Those prices show here, and on item hover cards across the app, with how old they are. The addon only reads pages you open; it never searches, bids or posts.
- **Reputation:** each character's factions with standing and progress, Alliance and Horde alike.
- **Cooldowns:** transmutes, Mooncloth and the Salt Shaker, and when each character's is ready again.

None of this leaves your PC; the ledger is not part of community sharing.

**In game:** `/bf ledger` shows gold across your characters and when this character's bank and mailbox were last seen. `/bf prices on|off|status` controls price memory.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once, then open your bank and mailbox once on each character.
