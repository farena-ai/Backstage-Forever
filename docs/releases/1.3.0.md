# Backstage Forever 1.3.0 — Deeper guidance

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**Your class's big moments.** Characters → Talents now lists the class quests and unlocks that matter: Bear Form for druids, your first pet for hunters, totems for shamans, demons for warlocks, stances for warriors, and more, with the next ones highlighted.

**Can you afford training?** Open your class trainer's window once in game. From then on, Now shows what the next visits cost and whether your gold covers the next one.

**Saving for your mount.** Open the riding trainer and a mount vendor once, and Ledger → Gold & prices adds them up into a riding-and-mount goal you can save with one click.

**Reputation, faster.** Ledger → Reputation now shows, for each faction, the quests you haven't done that give the most reputation, where they start, and what the next standing brings.

**Boss tips.** The Instance Atlas has a short tip for each boss of the leveling dungeons, and the game shows it the first time you target one. `/bf boss` repeats it.

Windows installs update by themselves. On a Mac, download the new zip and replace the app in Applications. Your journal stays. Type `/reload` in game once after updating.
