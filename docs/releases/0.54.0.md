# Backstage Forever 0.54.0 — The finished look

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**The Adventure Compass at a glance.** In game, the Compass's text now sits neatly inside its painted frame, with the title centred under the crest. Press the **−** button and it becomes a small live summary: your name, level and zone; an XP bar with your rested XP in blue; how much XP you're earning per hour and roughly when you'll level; how many quests you have and how many are ready to hand in; and your next step (from the guided route when you're following it). German clients see it in German.

**Every page reviewed.** We went through every page of the app in English and German and fixed what didn't look right:

- The **Zones** page's tiles are readable again: the zone names had turned dark on dark.
- **Dungeons** only suggests quests that fit the dungeon's level before you go in (no more beginner crafting quests for Sunken Temple), and the list is easier to read.
- **Your gear** on the Characters page shows each item's quality in words and colour (Uncommon, Rare…), and the status above it is in plain words.
- Save times read like a date ("Nov 15, 2026, 3:01 PM"), not computer code.
- The menu fits on laptop screens without scrolling, small print is never smaller than 11px, and stray decorations at the edges of cards are gone.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW (or restart it) so the addon picks up the new Compass.
