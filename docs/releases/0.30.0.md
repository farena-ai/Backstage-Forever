# Backstage Forever 0.30.0 — loot, crafts and every node you've found

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Dungeon loot browser.** Instance Atlas → **Loot** on any dungeon card shows each boss's recorded drops:
- item icons with quality colours, slot and drop chance
- an **Upgrade for you** badge, worked out against the gear your character wears
- a star to put an item on your **wish list**; starred items show on the dungeon card
- a "3 upgrades for you" chip on each dungeon card

Loot comes from what Forever players have actually seen drop (Efficient Games data, CC BY 4.0). Bosses nobody has recorded yet say so; more fill in as the community data grows.

**Hover cards for items and NPCs.** Rest the mouse on an item (loot, quest rewards, vendor wares) for its stats, whether it's an upgrade and which boss drops it. Rest on an NPC (World Map lists, Now's trainer) for who they are, what they teach or sell, and where they stand.

**Professions that guide you.**
- Every recipe shows its skill-up colour: orange, yellow, green or grey.
- **Best next recipe:** the one most likely to give a skill point that you can make soonest.
- **Skill-up countdown:** an estimate of crafts to your next point and to your trainer's cap.
- **Shopping list:** everything you need for your next crafts, with which vendor sells it and where.
- In game: `/bf prof` shows the same.

**Gathering memory.** The addon now remembers up to 500 herb, ore and chest spots per character (chests are new). The World Map has a **gathering heat** layer with a count per zone, and `/bf nodes` in game lists your nearest remembered spots with an arrow to the closest.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once. Open your profession window once so the addon can read your recipes' colours.
