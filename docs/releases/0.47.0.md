# Backstage Forever 0.47.0 — plan tonight with the companion

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**"What should I do tonight?"** Ask the companion and you get a plan in order. For example:

1. Empty your bags first: only 2 free slots.
2. Hand in Call of Water to Islen Waterseer, south-east.
3. Then clear The Barrens: 14 quests left there at your level.
4. If you find a group: Wailing Caverns, right for your level; pick up Leaders of the Fang first.
5. Put on the Barbed Leggings you already carry.

It also tells you when rested XP is full on another character and that character is worth playing tonight. "Who should I play?" works too.

The companion builds the plan from what the app already knows: the next step from Now, the Zones page, your Sheet, Dungeons and All characters, and this session's pace. With an AI model chosen, the model gets the same facts and can explain or adjust the plan. Without one, the quick answer gives the plan straight away.

Your character needs to be shared with the companion (the switch in the Companion) for any of this to be used.
