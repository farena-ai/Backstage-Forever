# Backstage Forever 1.12.0 — Field Dispatch

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: field-dispatch-update-recap | new | desktop | none -->
- **Catch up after an update.** The illustrated Field Dispatch covers the releases you skipped when the app knows your previous installed version. Choose **Not now** to defer it or **Got it** to acknowledge it. A fresh install or an unknown predecessor does not claim an upgrade range.
<!-- change: field-dispatch-settings-history | new | desktop | none -->
- **Find past changes in Settings.** Open **Settings → Updates → What’s New** to search the bundled release history offline, even after dismissing the recap.
<!-- change: forever-evidence-for-offline-instance-prep | improved | desktop | companion -->
- **Recognized dungeon and raid preparation questions stay offline.** Ask desktop Companion a question such as `Dungeon prep for The Deadmines?`. The reply uses eligible World of Warcraft: Forever evidence and identifies gaps rather than filling them with facts from another edition. Saved-character context is optional. These exchanges are not sent to a model or reused in provider history or Companion Memory.
<!-- change: forever-preparation-scope | compatibility | desktop,addon | none -->
- **Know what this covers.** Saved preparation still belongs in desktop Companion, not the in-game Ask Panel. This change does not qualify general Companion Q&A as Forever evidence. Atlas remains a separately labeled reference, not a substitute for verified Forever facts.
