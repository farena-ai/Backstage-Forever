# Backstage Forever 0.33.0 — live moments

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

0.33 also brings the quiet live link from 0.32 (whose installer build didn't finish): the strip the addon draws shrinks from 456×12 to **84×4 pixels**, sits in any corner (`/bf live pos`), and has a sturdy safe mode for remote desktop (`/bf live safe`).

**The app now reacts to what happens in your game**, with a painted medallion for each moment:
- **Death screen:** what killed you, its level and whether it was elite, how much of the damage it did, what to change next time, and how to get back to your body.
- **Boss, elite and rare kills.** Killing a dungeon's last boss shows **Dungeon cleared**.
- **Profession milestones:** every 25 skill points, and when you hit your rank's cap, which rank comes next and where to train it.
- **New zone, flight path learned, an upgrade equipped, a quest chain finished.**

Moments show one at a time, never over the level-up celebration, and a **Recent moments** card on Now keeps the last few. Turn them off, or just some kinds, in Settings → Game connection.

**In game:** `/bf moments on|off` controls quiet toasts for kills and profession milestones. They wait until combat ends.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once.
