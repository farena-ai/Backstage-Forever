# Backstage Forever 0.49.0 — painted frames, welcome tour and faction themes

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**In game: painted frames.** The Adventure Compass has a carved leather-and-brass frame with a compass crest on top, replacing the plain grey dialog border. The BF Route step guide (`/bf route`) is now a parchment scroll between two wooden rods, written in dark ink.

**Welcome tour.** New players see a painted splash when they first open the app, then three short cards: what the app does for you, your field journal, and one-click setup. Help → Field guide → **Replay the welcome tour** shows it again anytime.

**Horde and Alliance themes.** The app takes on your character's side. The Horde or Alliance crest appears beside the Backstage Forever name and on every page banner, and the banner edges and labels take your side's colours. Switch characters and the theme follows. Both sides get the same care.

All of the art is original, painted for Backstage Forever on our own computer. None of it comes from the game.

**After updating:** quit WoW and start it again once. This version adds a new art folder, and WoW only sees new folders after a restart (`/reload` isn't enough).
