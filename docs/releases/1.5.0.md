# Backstage Forever 1.5.0 — Graphics, part 2

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**Maps that move.** When you open a target on the World Map, the line to it now flows toward it and the target pulses. Your trail is footprints. (Still pictures if your system asks for less motion.)

**Bosses you can see.** Every dungeon and raid on the Instance Atlas shows a painted silhouette of its final boss, and the loot sits on velvet trays.

**Big moments.** Your own mount, levels 40 and 60, Artisan, Exalted and the other top badges get a big banner, and finishing a zone is celebrated once.

**In game too.** The Adventure Compass shows your next badge, and after you log in the chat reminds you about your other characters: rested XP full, a cooldown ready, mail about to expire.

Windows installs update by themselves. On a Mac, download the new zip and replace the app in Applications. Your journal stays.
