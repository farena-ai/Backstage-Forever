# Backstage Forever 0.56.0 — Ready for the public

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**A proper front page.** The project page now says what Backstage Forever does, in the game and on your desktop, with a picture of the Now page and download links.

**Clear about privacy.** [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) explains in plain words what the app reads (only your own game folder), what stays on your PC (everything, unless you choose an online AI) and how to delete it all (Help → Field guide → Delete all local data).

**Getting started, rewritten** for today's app: install, first launch, the addon and the live link. [Compatibility](../COMPATIBILITY.md) shows what is checked in the real game and what isn't yet.

**For anyone who wants to help:** a security policy for reporting problems privately, a contributing guide, a code of conduct and issue templates for bugs and translations.

**The app itself** works as in 0.55.1. Mac downloads are on this release too.
