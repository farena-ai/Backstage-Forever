# Backstage Forever 0.38.0 — ask from the game, a hidden live link

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Ask the companion without leaving WoW.** A new owl button on the edge of your minimap (drag it anywhere around the edge) opens a question box. Type up to 200 characters and press Ask, or type `/bf ask <your question>` in chat. The answer appears in a small panel over the top-right of your game. It never takes the keyboard away from WoW and closes by itself. "Where is…" and "how do I get to…" questions are answered instantly from the built-in reference; anything else goes to the AI you chose in the app. Nothing is sent until you press Ask, and nothing during combat. `/bf ask hide` hides the button. Your questions and answers also appear in the app's Companion conversation. The panel works over WoW in windowed or "fullscreen (windowed)" mode, not exclusive fullscreen.

**A live link you can barely see.** The colour strip is gone. In its place is a one-pixel dark line along the top-left edge of the game, and the app now reads it from the WoW window itself, so the link keeps working while other windows are on top of the game. Questions from the owl button arrive in about a second. When WoW is minimized, the app says live updates are paused, keeps your last position and how long ago that was, and picks up again by itself.

On remote desktop, colours get changed on the way, and the app will tell you to type `/bf live safe` for the sturdier visible strip. `/bf live hairline` switches back.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once (exit and restart WoW if the owl button doesn't appear), then `/bf live hairline` if you had switched to safe mode before.
