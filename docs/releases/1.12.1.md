# Backstage Forever 1.12.1 — Forever dungeon and raid questions

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: forever-instance-facts-offline | fixed | desktop,addon | companion -->
- **Recognized dungeon and raid fact questions stay local.** Ask Companion `Where is #dm?` or `What bosses are in Scarlet Monastery?`. If there is no verified current Forever answer, it says so rather than using Classic Atlas facts or asking a model. Atlas remains a separate reference.
<!-- change: forever-instance-travel-origin | fixed | desktop,addon | none -->
- **Travel keeps the destination separate from the starting point.** A dungeon named as the origin is not treated as the destination, and an unresolved explicit origin does not silently fall back to your character’s location.
<!-- change: forever-historical-instance-observations | compatibility | desktop,addon | none -->
- **Historical observations are not a current boss list.** `Historical encounters for #sm` and `Historical encounters for #rfk` label the Forever build and collection window. These are not proof of access or of your character’s defeats.
