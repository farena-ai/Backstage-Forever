# Backstage Forever 0.24.0 — how to actually do it

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Also includes everything from 0.23.0** (reward picks with icons and stats, the talent coach, in-game upgrade tooltips, data coverage, class crests). The 0.23.0 installer was never published because its release build hit slow-runner test timeouts, so 0.24.0 is the update to install.

**Every quest says how.** Quest pages now open with **How to do it**: plain steps that say whether to kill something or pick something up, and where. Each step is built from the best thing we know: what you've looted yourself, what the community has seen drop ("dropped by Young Goretusk, 40% of kills", or "found in 'Bottle', click them"), the creatures' levels (with elite warnings), and the quest's own text. Guesses are marked as guesses. Ask the companion "how do I do this?" and it now answers the same way.

**The addon learns as you play.** It saves the text of the quests in your log and remembers where quest items came from when you loot them. `/bf how` in game tells you how to do your tracked quest.

**See where objectives happen.** The World Map shades each objective's area in the colour of how dangerous it is for your level, and marks elites. Click an area for details.

**Ready for dungeons.** Each dungeon card shows whether your level fits, which quests to pick up first (with map buttons) and the group you'll need.

**Know your weakest gear.** Characters shows each slot with its icon and stats, how it holds up at your level, and what to upgrade first.

After updating, type `/reload` in game.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.24.0 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
