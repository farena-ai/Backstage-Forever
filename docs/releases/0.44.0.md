# Backstage Forever 0.44.0 — mini Now

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Your next step, beside the game.** On Now, click **Mini Now**. A small window opens and stays on top of your game. It shows one thing to do next:

- a finished quest to hand in, and who to;
- otherwise the nearest objective you still need, like "Raptor Head 3/12";
- otherwise a quest to pick up nearby, or the zone to head for.

It also says which way to go from where you're standing ("south-east", "a short way north", "in The Barrens"). Under the step are your experience bar with rested XP and your pace this session: levels per hour and minutes to the next level.

Drag it by its top bar to wherever suits you, and close it with ✕. It never takes your keyboard away from the game. With the live link on it follows you as you play; without it, it shows your last save.
