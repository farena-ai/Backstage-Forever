# Backstage Forever 1.9.0 — Badges and Classic raid tips

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: confirmed-dungeon-badges | new | desktop | badges -->
- **Dungeon badges:** earn First dungeon victory and Dungeon veteran from one or five distinct saved dungeon boss wins. Entering an instance, raid wins, and reference entries don’t count.
<!-- change: reviewed-quest-chain-badges | new | desktop | badges -->
- **Quest-chain badges:** three reviewed paths now have badges: The Defias Brotherhood (Westfall), A Husband’s Revenge, and The Missing Shipment. Every step must appear in the same character’s saved quest history.
<!-- change: faction-reputation-medals | new | desktop | badges -->
- **Faction medals:** earn separate Revered and Exalted medals for Stormwind, Orgrimmar, and Timbermaw Hold. A faction hidden during the latest scan cannot establish new medal progress; expand it in WoW and reload or log out to save a fresh observation. The new badges and medals are desktop-only.
<!-- change: classic-molten-core-and-bwl-tips | new | desktop,addon | atlas -->
- **Classic raid tips:** reviewed Molten Core and Blackwing Lair boss tips are available in the desktop Instance Atlas and in-game with `/bf boss`. These are reference notes, not live encounter validation.
<!-- change: reputation-journal-rollback | compatibility | desktop | none -->
- **Journal compatibility:** reputation scans saved in the same second are kept in order. Back up your journal before rolling back to v1.8.0: ordinary saves there preserve the newer awards, but accepting a newer game snapshot in v1.8.0 can lose them. See the [rollback and recovery guide](https://github.com/farena-ai/Backstage-Forever/blob/v1.9.0/docs/SUPPORT-AND-RECOVERY.md#returning-to-v180).

The Windows installer is timestamped and signed by Frank Arena. Automated qualification uses isolated fixtures; it does not include a live WoW session or an installed upgrade on a player’s machine.
