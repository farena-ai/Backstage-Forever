# Backstage Forever 0.59.0 — The companion understands more

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**Talk to it like a player.** The companion now gets "wheres the apothecary dude in xroads", "fastest way to tb from xroads", "never been to uc, how do i get there from splintertree", "who teaches guns", "got all 15 centaur bracers, who wants them?", "kuray'bin wants me to kill outrunners, where are they hiding" and "what skill do i need for embossed leather gloves". All of them are answered from WoW Forever's own data, with the town and zone.

**Tested on questions it had never seen.** A second set of 60 questions, written without looking at how the companion works, went from 56.7% answered well to 100%.

**Travel that knows the rules.** It tells you when a zeppelin belongs to the other faction, when there's no direct zeppelin at all, and how far a town is on foot when you're already in its zone.

**The addon on its own.** Every release now includes `BackstageForever-Addon-0.59.0.zip` for manual installs. Backstage Forever is getting ready for CurseForge, Wago and WoWInterface.
