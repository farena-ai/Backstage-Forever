# Backstage Forever 0.22.1 — level up like you mean it

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Levelling up is an event now.** When you gain a level, the app bursts into gold: light rays, fireworks, confetti, and the winged crest with your number ticking up under a shining LEVEL UP! title. A short fanfare plays; you can switch it off and the app remembers.

The celebration also tells you what's new: the skills waiting at your trainer, with a button that shows the trainer on the map. At levels 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 it adds a milestone note, like talents unlocking at 10 or riding at 40.

**In game,** the level-up banner is bigger, gold-edged and stays up longer.

After updating, type `/reload` in game.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.22.1 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
