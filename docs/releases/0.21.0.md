# Backstage Forever 0.21.0 — repairs, flight paths and a fresh coat of paint

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Never get caught in broken gear.** When your gear wears down to a quarter, Backstage Forever tells you, and tells you again if something breaks. `/bf repair` puts an arrow on the nearest vendor who can repair, for both Horde and Alliance. The addon also remembers every repair vendor you open.

**Collect your flight paths.** The addon notices which flight paths you know when you open a flight map or talk to a flight master. Walk past one you haven't learned and you get a gentle reminder. `/bf flights` shows what you have in the current zone.

**More for Alliance.** Vendors went from Horde-only to both factions: 291 vendors, including Elwynn Forest, Stormwind, Westfall, Teldrassil and Darnassus. Alliance flight masters now show on the in-game map.

**A reminder to reload.** If the game is still running the previous addon after an update, Now asks you to type `/reload`.

**A fresh coat of paint.** Original hand-painted art across Now, Help and the level-up celebration, plus a crest for each Commands group.

After updating, type `/reload` in game. Help → Commands explains every command.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.21.0 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
