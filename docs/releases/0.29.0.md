# Backstage Forever 0.29.0 — know more, faster

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Hover cards everywhere.** Rest your mouse on any quest in the app (on Now, in your quest log, in lists) and a card pops up with:
- its level
- how to do it
- who gives it
- the rewards, with their icons

Click it to open the full quest as before.

**What killed you?** After a death, a short review pops up in game:
- the enemy that did most of the damage, and how much of it
- that enemy's level, and whether it was elite
- one plain tip for next time, like "pull one at a time", "that was 3 levels above you" or "move out of that spell"

**Session stats.** `/bf stats` shows your fights this session: damage, healing, average damage per second, your best fight and your last one. It reads only your own combat log.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once so the game loads the new addon file.
