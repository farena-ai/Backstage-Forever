# Backstage Forever 0.45.0 — spoken steps

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Hear your next step.** On Now, click **Speak steps**. From then on, whenever your next step changes, Backstage Forever reads it aloud with your computer's own Windows voice. For example: "Hand in Call of Water to Islen Waterseer, south-east", or "Raptor Thieves: Raptor Head 3/12, a short way north."

It's the same step that Mini Now shows: a quest to hand in first, then the nearest objective, then a quest to pick up. **Say again** repeats it. Click the button again to stop.

It's off until you switch it on, and the app remembers your choice. The voice runs on your computer, so nothing is sent anywhere. If Windows has no voice installed, the button tells you.
