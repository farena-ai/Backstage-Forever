# Backstage Forever 0.19.0 — a coach at your side

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Help at the moments new players get lost.**
- Target something dangerous and a plain warning appears above its portrait: "Elite: bring a group", "Level 16, 6 above you: avoid it", or "Too low to give experience".
- Die, and Backstage Forever explains Release Spirit, then puts an arrow on your body.
- Level up, and a pop-up names your nearest class trainer. `/bf train` points the arrow there.
- Finish a quest, and you see how many more like it you need to level.

**Sell junk in one click.** Vendor windows get a button that lists your grey items and what they're worth. It sells only when you click it.

**Know your pace.** `/bf xp` in game, and the new **This session** card on Now, show your XP per hour, time to the next level and rested bonus. Only time you actually play counts.

**A friendlier start.** Now shows a **Get set up** checklist that ticks itself off. The Field guide opens with "New to WoW? Start here" and a pocket glossary of the words you'll see.

**A little celebration.** Level-ups get confetti in the app. The World Map draws **your path this session**, with a star where you levelled and a cross where you died.

After updating, type `/reload` in game. `/bf help` lists every command.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.19.0 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
