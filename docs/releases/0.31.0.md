# Backstage Forever 0.31.0 — every page earns its place

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**One look, everywhere.** Every page now opens with its own painted banner (twelve new paintings) and uses the same parchment cards as Now. Quests, Professions, Plans, Party, Characters and Dungeons moved over from the older dark styles, and the Companion has its own owl crest.

**Adventure, rebuilt.** It no longer repeats Now. The banner shows your character, quests ready to hand in and goals done; the coach, quest plan and goals sit in cards with step art.

**Every link goes where it says.**
- The dungeon card opens Dungeons, the profession card opens Professions, and the saved-location card opens the World Map on that zone, with the zone's own painting.
- The coach's button follows its advice: bag space sends you to a vendor and `/bf junk`, crafting to Professions, quests to your quest plan.
- "Company" is now "Plans" and "Quest Guide" is now "Quests" everywhere.
- A new check clicks every Adventure, Help and Characters button and confirms where it lands.

**Real information instead of filler.** Repeated disclaimers became one small "Community data · may differ in game" note, marketing lines are gone, trainers show their zone instead of source counts, and Help → Field guide is rewritten as how-to steps.

**One set of class art** on every page.
