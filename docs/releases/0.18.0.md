# Backstage Forever 0.18.0 — Now, and a guide on your in-game map

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Now: what to do next.** The app opens on a new home page that works out your next move from your quest log, your level and your live position:

- **Do this next:** hand in finished quests at the nearest NPC, train new class skills (and where), continue your nearest quest with the next objective, or head to the zone that fits you best.
- **Where to level:** zones for your level and faction, with how many quests you can pick up there and the town to base from. Quests seen in play on WoW Forever override the reference level ranges.
- **Quests to pick up** in your best zone, each opening in Quests.
- **Class training:** what's new for you now, and what comes at your next training level.
- **Quest log:** hand-ins first, then open quests with progress.

It follows the live link: change zone, level up or finish an objective and it updates. "Show on map" opens the World Map on that NPC.

**A guide on WoW's own map.** After updating, type `/reload` in game. A **BF Guide** panel sits under BF Layers on the world map. It shows:

- quests you can pick up now, checked live against your level, faction, completed quests and quest log
- hand-ins for quests you've finished
- your class trainer and weapon masters
- profession trainers
- flight masters

These use WoW's own interface icons. Click any pin for WoW's waypoint arrow, which also shows on the minimap. Everything hides in combat. Each layer can be turned off.

**`/bf next`** points WoW's arrow at the nearest hand-in, or else the nearest quest to pick up.

Quest and NPC positions are community observations (Efficient Games, CC BY 4.0), so coverage grows as people play. Zone level ranges are a reviewed reference.
