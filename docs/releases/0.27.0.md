# Backstage Forever 0.27.0 — free AI for everyone

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Turn on free AI, in one click.** The Companion page has a new **Free AI companion** card. Click **Turn on free AI** and the app downloads a small AI model that suits your PC and runs it on your own computer:
- The standard model is Qwen3 4B, about 2.3 GB; the light model, Qwen2.5 1.5B, is about 1 GB.
- A progress bar shows the download, and Pause lets you stop and pick up later where you left off.
- It's free, private and works offline. There's no account, no key, and nothing else to install.
- Every download is checked against a fixed fingerprint before it's used.
- It uses your graphics card when it can and your processor when it can't.
- **Remove** gives the disk space back.

**Or use a free cloud key.** On a slower PC, open "Slow PC? Use a free cloud key instead". Click **Get a free key** for Google Gemini or Groq, paste it, and the app picks a good model for you. Free cloud keys come with daily limits, and Google may use free-tier questions to improve its products.

**Upcoming training.** Now's training card shows your next three training levels and what each brings, with each spell's icon from your own game install.

**Supplies and class helpers in game.**
- **Ammo:** warns when arrows or bullets run low.
- **Food and drink:** warns when you're running out.
- **Pets:** tells you when your pet is unhappy.
- **Soul shards:** warns when a warlock runs low.
- **Poisons:** reminds rogues to apply poison before a dungeon.

Each reminder pops up once, when the problem starts. `/bf supplies` shows everything at once, and `/bf supplies off` silences the reminders.

**Living headers.** The Now header slowly drifts across its painted scene, with embers rising, and zone banners pan gently. They stay still if Windows is set to reduce motion.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once so the game loads the new addon file.
