# Backstage Forever 0.51.0 — route review

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Review the guided routes by hand.** On Adventure → Guided route, click **Start reviewing**. Pick any race and go through its route, 40 steps at a time. For each step:

- ✓ keeps it, marked as reviewed;
- ✗ leaves it out of the route;
- ↑ and ↓ move it earlier or later;
- ↺ undoes your change.

Your changes show in the app straight away and are saved on this computer. When you're happy with a route, click **Export review**. It saves a `reviews.json` file, and once that file is in the project the next release builds your review into the in-game guide (`/bf route guide`).

The coverage table shows how many steps of each route have been reviewed.
