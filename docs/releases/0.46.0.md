# Backstage Forever 0.46.0 — zones, replay and journey picture

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Adventure → Zones.** Every zone you can level in, from 1 to 60, for your own faction (Horde and Alliance alike, plus the contested zones). Each zone is coloured by its level band and has a ring showing how many of its quests you've done, like "Durotar 18/22". A ★ marks zones you've finished (90% of their quests), a ◆ marks zones you've visited, and zones at your level have a gold edge. At the top, the page suggests the next zone worth clearing. Click any zone to open it on the World Map.

**Replay your session.** On the World Map, click ▶ to watch your path this session draw itself again on the zone you're looking at. Deaths and level-ups appear at the moment they happened.

**Save a picture.** Click 🖼 to save a hand-drawn-style picture of that path: the zone in sepia, your route in ink, where you started, died and levelled, with the zone name, your levels and time played. You choose where the PNG goes. It's made from your own path, with no game art beyond your own install's map.

The path is kept while the app is open, so a replay covers your current session.
