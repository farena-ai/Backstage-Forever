# Backstage Forever 0.8.0 — Adventure Company

Your collected characters and shared plans, together. Company helps you choose a useful character, organize a quest session or dungeon checklist, and recognize new saved quest progress without an AI account.

**[Download the Windows installer](https://github.com/farena-ai/Backstage-Forever/releases/download/v0.8.0/Backstage-Forever-0.8.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe)** · [Adventure Company guide](https://github.com/farena-ai/Backstage-Forever/blob/v0.8.0/docs/ADVENTURE-COMPANY.md)

## Your characters, together

Open **Company** for character cards with class/faction presentation, level, last-save labels, ready turn-ins, private goals and available bag/equipment facts. Counts describe collected observations, with unknown data kept distinct from an empty quest log. A suggested character explains the saved facts behind it. Choose its adventure action yourself; browsing Company does not switch your character or change following.

Only characters visited with the addon enabled can be collected. Company uses existing saved profiles; another sign-in is not required.

## Shared journeys

Create a blank plan or an editable quest-session/dungeon-preparation checklist. Assign steps across characters, leave them unassigned while deciding, or add a quest from a chosen character's saved log. Text edits save on blur; checkboxes and assignments save immediately with visible feedback. Private character journals and existing goals remain intact.

Journeys work locally and are included in deliberate backups. The initial limits are 20 plans with 50 steps each. Removing a character preserves its steps for your review. Converting a tracked quest to another character makes it a manual step instead of silently transferring game evidence.

Templates are your editable planning prompts, not class-role assignments, gear scores or dungeon eligibility verdicts.

## Quest steps that show their evidence

Tracking starts from the selected saved capture. A newer compatible save can prove a new turn-in or a first transition into a known completed-ID list. A ready-to-turn-in quest is not yet a proved turn-in, and disappearance from the log does not prove completion. Repeatables already historically complete require a new observed turn-in; unknown history becoming available is insufficient by itself.

Manual checks and observed completions have distinct labels. Observed evidence is retained when recent history rolls over, with its build/date qualification. Older saves, changed source identity/build, missing data and orphaned assignments do not become invented progress. Remove/re-add a quest to track another run.

The desktop still receives normal logout/exit saves. A voluntary reload can save immediately if you specifically need fresh desktop context. There is no routine reload requirement for the live Compass, and tracking does not send an AI request or control gameplay.

## Quiet preparation in game

Adventure Compass adds a quiet bag-space line when existing supported current observations report two or fewer free slots in a positive bag capacity. It clears when space improves or the session/character changes. Missing or zero capacity is not treated as a full bag.

Native combat hiding, dragging, collapse, close, source-labelled guidance and explicit waypoint controls remain. Use **/bf show** or **/bf hide**. The cue has no sound, message or automatic game action, and works with the desktop app closed.

## Activate and keep your data

Update/restart BF normally and let it prepare the companion files. Shared plans can use your existing saved characters. Prepare before launching WoW where possible; an already installed addon can load updated files with one voluntary **/reload**, or on the next game launch. A newly added addon that is not listed may require a game restart.

Native progress updates do not require repeated reloads. Logout/exit saves for the desktop. Your profiles, notes and encrypted provider connections remain; do not reset data to upgrade. Shared journeys stay out of automatic AI context. Anything you deliberately type into an AI question is sent when you submit it.

Old backups without journeys restore an empty shared-plan collection after the existing replacement confirmation. Malformed data preserves current state. Use a current BF version to restore a newer backup.
