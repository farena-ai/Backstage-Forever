# Backstage Forever 1.25.0 — Your route, more data

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: route-variants-editor | new | desktop,addon | guided-route -->
- **Choose and edit your route.** Adventure → Guided route offers five routes per character: Solo, Duo, Dungeon-heavy, Fewest trips and Careful. Each card says what it changes and what it cannot know. You can move steps, skip them, add notes and your own steps, and share the result as a short code. `/bf route guide` walks your edited steps in game, with your note on the step.

<!-- change: challenge-runs | new | desktop | badges -->
- **Challenge runs.** Characters → Journey → Challenge run: one life, no auction house, self-found, no dungeons below a level, or a rule of your own. A rule is marked broken only from a saved record; everything else is shown as self-declared.

<!-- change: vendor-prices-stock | improved | desktop | workshop -->
- **Vendor prices and limited stock.** The recipe book and skill path now show what sellers charge and which items are in limited supply, from a refreshed community dataset (6,337 priced listings).

<!-- change: flights-boats | improved | desktop | none -->
- **Better travel planning.** Flight points have their real names, every one has a place and every connection a price, read from your own game files. Boats and zeppelins are in the planner, so trips between continents can be planned. A ride shows "about N min plus the wait": ride times are worked out from the path, not timed.

<!-- change: routes-to-60 | improved | desktop,addon | guided-route -->
- **Routes reach level 60** for both factions (they stopped at 55–56), and four more Alliance zones have enough placed quests to be useful.

<!-- change: reputation-amounts | fixed | desktop | ledger -->
- **Reputation amounts corrected.** The planner used reward sizes from older versions of the game. WoW Forever pays less (50, 75, 100, 150, 200), so earlier plans overstated gains.

<!-- change: trainer-levels | improved | desktop | none -->
- "At the trainer" fills in skill levels nobody had recorded, from your game files.

<!-- change: discord-status | new | desktop | none -->
- A Discord status option exists under Journey but stays off until the project has a Discord application id.

<!-- change: data-limits-125 | compatibility | desktop,addon | none -->
Still thin, because nobody has recorded it yet: Dun Morogh and several other Alliance zones, Rogue trainer skills, most recipe sources, places for most rare enemies, and flight times nobody has flown. Boats, zeppelins and the new route features have not been checked in the real game client.
