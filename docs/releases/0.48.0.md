# Backstage Forever 0.48.0 — past sessions

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Every session, remembered.** Characters → Journey has a new **Past sessions** card. Each time you play with the live link on, the app keeps the session: when it was, how long you played, your level at the start and end, levels gained, deaths and how many zones you went through.

At the top is a chart of levels gained per day for the last two weeks, with your totals: sessions, time played, levels and deaths.

A break of 30 minutes or more starts a new session, so the pace on Now and Mini Now starts fresh after dinner too.

The sessions stay on this computer, in the app's own data folder. **Delete all local data** on Help → Field guide removes them.
