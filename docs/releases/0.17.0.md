# Backstage Forever 0.17.0 — a World Map you can read

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Pins you can tell apart.** Each kind of place has its own shape and colour. Quests are shields: `!` to pick up, `?` to hand in. Objectives are red crosshairs and flight masters are blue diamonds. Trainers are hexagons: red with swords for class trainers, teal with a hammer for profession trainers. Vendors are coin squares, herbs are leaves, ore is rock, and your own finds carry a star. The layer list, search results, "Near you" and the details card all use the same icons.

**NPCs that tell you something.** Click an NPC and the card shows:
- who they are and which faction they serve
- the quests they give and take, marked for you: ready to hand in, in your log, available, level too low, or done
- for a class trainer, what you can learn now and later, or that they teach a different class
- everything a vendor sells
- other places they've been seen

Click a quest to open it in Quests.

**Your faction first.** NPCs who only serve the other faction are hidden unless you turn them on. When shown, they're dimmed and marked. They never appear on the radar.

**Better search.** Searching "hunter trainer" now finds the hunter trainer instead of the nearest cook.

**One map.** The old Adventure → Map grid is gone. The World Map covers everything it did, on real terrain with your live position.

This is the first step of a larger plan to turn Backstage Forever into a live leveling advisor. Next come leveling data (zones by level, quest hubs, a suggested path), then a "Now" home page that tells you what to do next, then an in-game map and minimap built from the same data.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.17.0 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
