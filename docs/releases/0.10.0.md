# Preparation and Professions 0.10 milestone

This is a development milestone within the authorized series through 0.12, not a separately published update. Final combined delivery is 0.12.3;1.0 remains held for owner acceptance.

Preparation adds a class/faction-themed workshop for owned professions, learned recipes and carried materials. Choose a saved recipe and craft batches to see source-dated requirements and material observations. Exact shortfalls require complete applicable backpack/four-bag counts; partial, older or unavailable counts remain qualified instead of becoming invented zeroes. Other characters' observations remain separate.

Add shopping checklist creates a deliberate manual Company plan from current trusted facts, with new main-owned IDs and one atomic save. It does not craft, buy, transfer items, infer a skill-up, mark a quest completed or automatically check steps from inventory. Recipe/craft preferences autosave per character. Native dropdowns remain readable; queued updates preserve held controls and dirty text.

The addon uses bounded public Forever-compatible profession/recipe/container APIs outside combat. Owned profession identity and learned recipes are checked; ambiguous optional/alternate/variable requirements remain unknown. Missing/protected APIs keep core character, quests, XP and native guidance useful. Recipe caches retain their actual observation dates and reset on identity/build/session/skill changes. No bank, account, guild or mail inventory is collected.

Rich recipe/reagent snapshots survive reload within unchanged byte and parser-node budgets. A large synthetic roster exposed a below-byte-limit but above-node-limit export; the corrected encoder measures both, removes optional workshop detail first and preserves character identities and old quest facts. An exact boundary of 200,000 parsed values and optional-core fallback are covered.

All development uses synthetic game folders/data and fake launch/inference paths. Real profession API behavior, recipes, materials and final graphics remain part of the owner's 0.12 hands-on check before 1.0. See PREPARATION.md, VALIDATION.md and OWNER-CHECK-012.md for usage and measured milestone evidence.
