# Backstage Forever 0.28.0 — your journey

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Characters → Journey.** A new page for each character:
- **Badge wall:** 20 original painted badges:
  - **Levels:** 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60.
  - **Quests:** 25, 100 and 500 completed.
  - **Exploring:** 5, 15 and 30 zones visited.
  - **Firsts:** first dungeon, flight, mount, group, Hearthstone trip, rested bonus, and even your first death.
  - **Speed:** a level in under an hour.

  Earned badges glow. Locked ones show exactly what's left ("15 / 20").
- **Speed splits:** how much play time each level took, from your own `/played`. Your best times are in gold, with a marker for your fastest character at that level.
- **Rested planner:** how much rested bonus you have, how much an inn adds overnight, and how long until it's full.

**New badge moments.** Earn a badge and a painted banner flips in with it, once per badge.

**The addon keeps a journal.** It now notes when you reach each level (with your play time, asked for quietly so nothing shows up in chat), your firsts, and the zones you visit. It all stays on your PC.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once. Speed splits start from your next level-up.
