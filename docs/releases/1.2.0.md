# Backstage Forever 1.2.0 — Badges in game, and what was half-built

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**Your badges, in game.** `/bf badges` shows how many you've earned and the three you're closest to. `/bf badges all` lists them. A new badge gets a small banner in game too.

**Gathering spots on the World Map.** Open the map (M) and turn on the BF Guide's Gathering spots layer: herbs, ore and chests you gathered before, greyed when they need more skill than you have. `/bf nodes` skips those too.

**Prices on your items.** Item tooltips show what you saw the item sell for at auction and what a vendor pays, and turn green when the Auction House clearly pays more. `/bf sell` goes through your bags and tells you what to auction and what to vendor. Nothing is ever sold or posted for you.

**Quest items say so.** An item a quest in your log needs says which quest and how many you have, like "Needed for Rites of the Earthmother: 3/8".

**Quest icons over enemies.** A small ! above enemies a quest still needs (nameplates on, V key). `/bf plates off` hides them.

**Gear sets in one click.** A Gear sets button beside your character window wears a saved set from your bags. Never in combat.

**Reputation badges unlock** from your saved reputations.

Windows installs update by themselves. On a Mac, download the new zip and replace the app in Applications. Your journal stays. Type `/reload` in game once after updating.
