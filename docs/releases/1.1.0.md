# Backstage Forever 1.1.0 — Badges you can look back on

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**A page for your badges.** The banner for a new badge only shows once. Now Characters → Badges keeps them all: every badge each character has earned, with the date, what's closest to earning next, and who else on your account has it. Click the banner when a new badge appears and it opens right on that badge.

**More to earn.** Fourteen new painted badges: profession skill (75, 150, 225 and 300), gold carried (10, 100 and 1000), reputation (Honored, Revered and Exalted), and account badges earned by all your characters together (2, 5 and 10 characters, and one for playing both Horde and Alliance).

Windows installs update by themselves. On a Mac, download the new zip and replace the app in Applications. Your journal stays.
