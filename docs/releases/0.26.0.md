# Backstage Forever 0.26.0 — follow the steps

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**A step-by-step guide in the game.** Type `/bf route` and a small frame shows one step at a time for the zone you're in: pick up these quests, do this objective, hand in to this NPC. The steps go in the order that walks the least, and quests you're already carrying come before new ones. The game's own arrow sits on the current step. The guide moves on by itself as you accept and hand in quests. `/bf route next` and `/bf route back` let you look ahead, and `/bf route arrow` turns the arrow off.

**Quest progress on enemies.** Hover an enemy and its tooltip says if it counts for one of your quests, and how far along you are ("Raptor Thieves: Raptor Head 3/12"). Drops count once you've looted that item from that kind of enemy.

**You are here.** Now opens its side column with a painted card for the zone you're in:
- its level range and whether it fits you
- how many quests there are for your level, and where most of them start
- the inn, the flight master and your class trainer
- repair vendors and profession trainers
- any elite or higher-level enemies to be careful of

Every row has a **Show on map** button that opens the map right on that spot.

**Zone arrival banners.** When you ride into a new zone, a painted banner sweeps in with its name, level range and quests for your level, both in the app and in the game.

**Painted map pins.** The World Map's quest givers, hand-ins, flight masters, trainers, vendors, herbs, ore and dungeons are now painted medallions, original art made for Backstage Forever. Orgrimmar and Stormwind get their own painted city banners.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once so the game loads the new addon file.
