# Backstage Forever 0.18.2 — live link that survives remote access

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Live works when you're remoted in.** The live strip at the top-left of the game now uses only full-on and full-off colours and slightly bigger cells, checked against white and black reference cells. Remote sessions, HDR, gamma or colour-profile changes, slight blur and resizing no longer stop the app from reading your position. The strip is a little longer than before.

**Nothing breaks while you update.** The app still reads the older strip, so it keeps working until the addon is updated.

After updating, type `/reload` in game.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.18.2 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
