# Backstage Forever 1.23.0 — Gear, groups and stories

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: stat-weights-gear-plan | new | desktop | none -->
- **Your stat weights and a gear plan.** Characters → Sheet → Gear plan: choose a preset for your class and role or set each stat's weight yourself. Every "Upgrade for you" in the app and the in-game atlas follows it. For each slot you see what you wear and up to three upgrades you can reach now or within three levels, with where each comes from, and you can pin one target per slot.

<!-- change: reputation-planner | new | desktop | ledger -->
- **Reputation planner.** Ledger → Reputation, "Reach a standing": points needed, hand-ins with how many of the goods you already hold, and quests that pay reputation. Amounts not recorded for WoW Forever use the classic table and say so.

<!-- change: keys-attunements | new | desktop | atlas -->
- **Keys and attunements.** A button on Dungeons & raids opens 14 key and attunement chains with the step you are on. The directions are classic reference text, not checked in WoW Forever.

<!-- change: share-story | new | desktop | badges -->
- **Share pictures and the story so far.** Characters → Journey has new views: Story so far, Timeline (with screenshots from the game's own Screenshots folder, read only), Races, Coach's notes and Stream overlay. One painted share card covers level-ups, badges, dungeon runs and bests; your name and realm appear only if you tick them.

<!-- change: races-overlay | new | desktop | none -->
- **Leveling races and a stream overlay.** Swap a short code with friends to compare level times on a private board. The stream overlay is a small page on your own computer only (off by default) that a streaming program can show.

<!-- change: ingame-panels | new | addon | none -->
- **New in-game panels.** `/bf tracker` lists your quests nearest first. `/bf run` shows bosses left, your quests and wishes still possible inside a dungeon. A banner appears when a wished item drops, `/bf wish` lists your wishes, and `/bf bags` lists upgrades, wishes and quest items in your bags. The tracker, run panel and bag marks are off until you turn them on.

<!-- change: group-tools | new | addon | none -->
- **Group and guild tools.** `/bf lfg` collects looking-for-group lines from channels you already joined. `/bf check` asks party members running the addon whether they are ready. `/bf mentor`, `/bf sr` (soft reserves, party only), `/bf guild` (off by default), `/bf shop` (your materials list beside the auction window) and `/bf addons` (steps aside for Questie, TomTom, Auctionator and others). Nothing is ever sent or searched for you: the addon only fills the chat or search box.

<!-- change: collections | new | desktop | ledger -->
- Ledger → Bags & bank lists the mounts, pets, tabards and unlearned recipes you hold.

<!-- change: next-step-fix | fixed | desktop | now -->
- Spoken next steps and the mini Now window no longer come up empty: they treated a good answer as a failure.

<!-- change: unverified-in-game-123 | compatibility | desktop,addon | none -->
The new in-game commands, panels and messages between players have not been checked in the real game client yet; they are tested against stand-ins. German text for the new features is a first translation.
