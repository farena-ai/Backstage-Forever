# Backstage Forever 1.18.1 — Fresh data

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: data-seven-builds | improved | desktop,addon | none -->
- The bundled data moves to a newer community snapshot and now covers seven game builds instead of four, including the current one. Quests with a known quest giver go from 910 to 1,536, quests with a known hand-in from 861 to 1,476, quest items with a known source from 485 to 809, bosses with recorded loot from 47 to 87, NPCs with a known place from 978 to 1,449, vendors from 291 to 417 (Alliance 62 to 113), trainers from 113 to 180, and flight masters from 22 to 38.

<!-- change: routes-to-55 | improved | desktop,addon | guided-route -->
- The guided routes reach level 55 or 56 for every race, up from 42, with 4,361 steps instead of 2,668. Dwarf and Gnome routes still start at level 10.

<!-- change: zone-advice-band | fixed | desktop | now -->
- Where to level no longer sends you away from a zone you are questing in because a neighbouring zone lists more quests, and a few level-60 holiday quests no longer stretch a low zone's level range.

<!-- change: companion-same-name | improved | desktop | companion -->
- When a word fits several creatures, the Companion answers with the one nearest you and names up to two others.

<!-- change: data-unknown-names | fixed | desktop,addon | none -->
- Creatures recorded with the game's placeholder name "Unknown" are left out.

<!-- change: data-scope | compatibility | desktop,addon | none -->
These are places and drops players have seen, not guarantees. Alliance starting zones are still thinner than Horde ones; Dun Morogh in particular has very little.
