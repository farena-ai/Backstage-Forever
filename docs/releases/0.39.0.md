# Backstage Forever 0.39.0 — painted message boxes

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Every message in game is painted now.** The see-through black boxes are gone. Each message Backstage Forever shows you in WoW is a solid painted banner like the zone banners: its own original painting, a gold frame, the title in gold and the text on a dark ribbon.

There are 14 paintings, one for each kind of message:

- flight master nearby
- quest complete
- level up (the big banner)
- you died
- run back to your body
- back on your feet
- gear wearing out or broken
- move your Hearthstone
- Backstage Forever is ready
- supplies (food, ammo, pet, soul shards, poisons)
- journey firsts
- "What happened" after a death
- elite, rare and boss kills
- profession milestones

Longer messages make the box taller instead of spilling out of it, and the painting is cropped to fit, never stretched.

Nothing else changes: same place on screen, same fade, still quiet in combat, and `/bf toasts` still turns them off (the chat line stays).

All art is original, painted locally for Backstage Forever.

**After updating:** quit WoW completely and start it again once. The game only picks up new art folders on a full restart, and `/reload` is not enough.
