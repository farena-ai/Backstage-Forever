# Backstage Forever 1.6.0 — Show it off, stock up

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**A picture of your badges.** Characters → Badges has a Save a picture button: your earned badges, painted on one card, ready to share.

**Shopping lists that check your bank.** Before you buy materials for your next skill-ups, the Professions page tells you where you already have them: your bank and mail, and your other characters' bags, banks and mail.

Windows installs update by themselves. On a Mac, download the new zip and replace the app in Applications. Your journal stays.
