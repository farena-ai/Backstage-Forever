# Backstage Forever 0.15.1 — pop-out radar

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Pop-out radar.** On the World Map, choose ◉ or **Track on radar** on any pin. A small, draggable, always-on-top window shows a north-up radar around your live position: your arrow, nearby quest markers, flight masters, trainers, vendors and herb and ore spots, plus town labels. Your tracked target shows as a ring, or as a gold arrow on the rim when it's out of range, with its direction and distance band underneath. Zoom in and out with − and +. Details pause during combat.

The radar only displays. It never sends input to the game. It has its own isolated window and messaging, and it needs the live game link to be on. Over WoW it appears in windowed or borderless fullscreen, not exclusive fullscreen.

Everything from 0.15.0 is unchanged: the World Map, `/bf find`, `/bf way` and the Instance Atlas. App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.15.1 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
