# Backstage Forever 0.15.0 — find anything, see where you are

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

This preview is about finding things and seeing them, on your desktop and inside WoW. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**

## World Map

A new **World Map** in the main navigation draws each zone as an original cartographer's map: parchment, grid, compass rose and town plates in a gilded frame. On top of it:

- **Your live arrow** with facing, a breadcrumb trail, and remembered paths, so the map fills in as you ride. The map follows your live zone when the live link is on, and shows your last saved spot otherwise.
- **Pins** for quest turn-ins, objectives and quests you could pick up (filtered to your level and faction), flight masters, trainers, vendors, herbs, ore and your own recorded gathering finds. Busy areas cluster; click a cluster to zoom.
- **Near you**: the closest useful places with direction ("southwest, short ride").
- **Find**: search every zone for an NPC, "flight master", "trainer", a vendor's wares, "herb" or "ore", nearest first.
- **Copy waypoint for WoW** on any pin. It copies `/bf way MapID X Y Name`; paste it in chat to get a native waypoint and the game's own arrow.

## /bf find, inside the game

`/bf find Cairne`, `/bf find flight master`, `/bf find trainer`, `/bf find peacebloom` and similar commands work with the desktop closed. They search a compiled, source-labelled table of about 900 places, prefer your current zone and the nearest point, set a waypoint, and list alternatives with ready `/bf way` commands. `/bf way MapID X Y [name]` sets any waypoint directly.

## Instance Atlas

**Adventure → Dungeons & raids** lists every classic dungeon and raid with its level band, zone, approximate entrance, boss roster, group roles and preparation checklist. Cards are coloured like in-game difficulty for your level, and your active quests are linked by their bosses; for example, Hidden Enemies appears under Ragefire Chasm. Bosses observed being killed in Forever are marked, and **New in Forever** shows instances seen in Forever that the classic list doesn't include. Each card can copy its entrance waypoint or open it on the World Map.

The baseline comes from the original game era and is labelled "verify in Forever". Observed Forever encounters are bundled community data (Efficient Games, CC BY 4.0).
