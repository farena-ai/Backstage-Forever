# Backstage Forever 1.29.0 — Backstage Live

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: live-panel | new | desktop | none -->
- **A live Guild & Party panel.** A small always-on-top window you can keep over the game. Events shows your guild's next events with sign-up counts by role and one-click answers. Party shows your current group and the plan. Board shows people looking for a group on your realm. It updates within seconds, and a line at the bottom says how many updates will reach the game at your next `/reload`.

<!-- change: guild-link | new | desktop,addon | none -->
- **Connect your guild in three steps.** A guild leader or officer opens Plans → Guild → "Connect our guild", then types `/bf guild link CODE` in game once. Every member's game then shows one prompt, and each member joins with one click in the app after reading what is shared. Each step has a status light and a test button. Leave, or delete the guild's data, at any time.

<!-- change: live-in-game | improved | addon | none -->
- **Faster in game.** Your events and sign-ups go out at once. Party members' class, level, role and ready state update for everyone as they change. An answered `/bf check` keeps itself current for 30 minutes. `/bf plan` shows your role and what to bring. Optional announcements to party or guild chat (`/bf announce`, off until you turn them on).

<!-- change: group-planner | new | desktop,addon | none -->
- **Group planner and regulars.** Plans → Party lets you put members into tank, healer and damage places with a note per place, and lists the people you have run with, with your own notes. The plan reaches the group through the game after a `/reload`.

<!-- change: realm-board | new | desktop,addon | none -->
- **Realm party board.** Post that you are looking for a group, or for more players, and browse others on your realm and faction, live. A listing shows your character's name, level, class, role and note to other Backstage Forever users on your realm for 30 minutes, and only after you agree. `/bf lfg list DUNGEON` posts from the game while the desktop app is running.

<!-- change: instant-replies | improved | desktop | none -->
- **Replies to your reports arrive at once** instead of at the next three-hour check.

<!-- change: discord-ready | new | desktop | none -->
- **Ready for Discord.** Events with sign-up buttons, reminders and run summaries in a guild's own Discord are built and will switch on in a coming update; until then the app says "Discord connection is not available yet".

<!-- change: live-limits | compatibility | desktop,addon | none -->
What is live and what is not: players in the same guild or party see each other's changes at once; the desktop panel updates in seconds; anything coming from outside the game (a change made on another computer, or later in Discord) reaches the running game at the next `/reload`, and one member's reload passes it on to every guildmate in game. Guild and party features are a separate, opt-in kind of sharing: they use character names, which anonymous statistics never do. Help → Guild & Party explains each step. These features have not been tried between real players in the game yet.
