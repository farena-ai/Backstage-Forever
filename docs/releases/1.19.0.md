# Backstage Forever 1.19.0 — Quest helper and Instance Atlas

<!-- whats-new: stable -->

<!-- change: instance-atlas-ingame | new | addon | none -->
- The Instance Atlas is now in the game. Type `/bf atlas` (or bind a key) for a painted window listing all 27 dungeons and raids, coloured by how each fits your level. Pick one for its painting, level band, side, an entrance button, the bosses in order, the loot players have seen each boss drop with its chance, and a short tip per boss. Quests shows the quests to bring for your side, marks the ones you have done or carry, and points to each giver.

<!-- change: minimap-quest-pins | new | addon | none -->
- Quest pins on the minimap: nearby quests to pick up, hand-ins, your class trainer, profession trainers and flight masters, with the same icons as the world map. A hand-in that is further away sits on the minimap's edge, in the direction to walk. `/bf minimap` switches it, and it has a switch in `/bf options`.

<!-- change: item-source-tooltips | new | addon | none -->
- Item tooltips say which enemies drop a quest item and how often, which objects hold it, and who sells an item, with the zone. Sellers for the other faction are left out.

<!-- change: available-and-chain | new | addon | none -->
- `/bf available` lists the quests you can pick up on the map you are on, nearest first, with distance and direction. `/bf chain` says which step of its chain each quest in your log is, and what comes next.

<!-- change: quest-helper-scope | compatibility | addon | none -->
Pins, drops and sellers come from places and loot players have seen, so a missing line does not mean something cannot be found. Not yet checked in the real game: where the minimap pins land, and the atlas window on screen. The dungeon paintings add about 5 MB to the addon.
