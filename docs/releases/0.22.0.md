# Backstage Forever 0.22.0 — dialed in, and the whole quest

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Show on map means exactly that.** Click Show on map anywhere on Now or on a quest page, and the World Map zooms to that one place:
- everything else fades back
- a gold route runs from you to it, with the direction and roughly how far
- a "Your goal here" card says what to do there, step by step

One click returns you to the full map.

**Quest pages tell the whole story.** Where to go comes first: who gives the quest, where each objective happens ("Kill 12 Southsea Brigand"), and who takes it back, each one click from the map. Rewards are cards: item names in their quality colours, money as coins, the experience you'll get at your level, reputation, and any spell the quest teaches. Quests link to what comes before and after.

**Now gets a fresh coat of paint.** Each kind of step has its own painted crest, and every zone in Where to level shows a painted vista of its landscape.

After updating, type `/reload` in game.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.22.0 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
