# Backstage Forever 0.23.0 — your game, read for you

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Every reward, fully explained.** Backstage Forever now reads item, spell and talent facts straight from your own WoW Forever install. Nothing is bundled or uploaded. Quest pages show each reward with its real icon, stats, damage and DPS. A green **Best choice for you** box names the pick for your class and level, and says why.

**In game, it points at the right reward.** On the quest reward screen, the best choice for you glows gold. Item tooltips tell you "Upgrade for you", "Not for you" or "Not an upgrade", and why. `/bf gear off` switches these off.

**A talent coach.** Characters → Talents shows your realm's real talent trees with a leveling path for your class. It shows what you should have by now, which point to spend next, and why that path suits leveling. From level 10, your level-up banner reminds you to spend the point.

**Know where the app is strong.** Help → Data coverage shows, zone by zone and per faction, how much the app knows.

**Little helpers:**
- Painted class crests.
- "No XP · safe to drop" on quests that no longer give experience.
- A tip in game when your Hearthstone should move.
- A list of every in-game helper under Settings → Game connection.

After updating, type `/reload` in game.

App, lockfile, addon TOC and capture metadata align at **0.23.0 preview**. **1.0 remains held for Frank's hands-on acceptance.**
