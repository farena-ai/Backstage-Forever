# Backstage Forever 0.57.2 — Professions give advice

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**Professions tell you what to craft.** 0.57.1 saved your recipes; now Professions uses them. It names the next recipe worth making, how many crafts to your cap, and what you're short of. For example: "Gather supplies for Heavy Woolen Gloves: 1 Fine Thread short". Colours stay current while your rank stays the same. After you skill up, open the profession once to refresh them.

**Grey is grey.** Recipes too easy for your rank show as grey instead of "colour not observed".

**Ready for 1.0.** Quest waypoints work with the coming 1.0 addon.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW, open each profession once, then `/reload` or log out so the game writes your save.
