# Backstage Forever 1.4.0 — Your evenings, planned

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena and Jason Babiuch. https://www.backstagecoding.com

**Tell it how long you have.** Ask the Companion "I have 90 minutes, what should I do?" and it plans the time: bags, your next step, a dungeon if there's room, and how many quests fit, with your pace.

**Quick answers.** "How do I get Bear Form?", "When do I get my Voidwalker?", "Any tips for VanCleef?", "Fastest way to Honored with Orgrimmar?" are answered at once, even without an AI service.

**This week.** Now shows how your week went in one line, and Characters → Journey has a This week card: levels, badges, time, gold and your fastest level, for every character.

**Reminders.** Now tells you when another character's rested XP is full, a profession cooldown is ready, or mail is about to expire.

**Routes that grow.** The guided route stops where our quest data stops. From now on, every quest you accept teaches the app where its giver stands, so routes past level 42 fill in as you play.

Windows installs update by themselves. On a Mac, download the new zip and replace the app in Applications. Your journal stays. Type `/reload` in game once after updating.
