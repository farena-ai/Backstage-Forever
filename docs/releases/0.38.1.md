# Backstage Forever 0.38.1 — talents the companion actually knows

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**The companion knows WoW Forever's talents.** Until now it was never given any talent data, so the AI guessed from other versions of the game and could invent talents that don't exist here. Now, when you ask about talents, specs or builds, it gets your class's real talent trees, read from your own WoW Forever install: every talent, its maximum rank, which tier it sits in and how many points that tier needs, plus your points at your level. It's told to answer only from that and to say so when something isn't there.

Common questions are answered instantly, with no AI and nothing sent anywhere: "what does Improved Renew do", "what talents should I take", "the first 7 Holy talents", "how many talent points at level 30". If the companion can't tell your class (character sharing off and no class named), it asks.

Also fixed: a remark like "Improved Renew only goes to 3/3" was mistaken for a travel question.

**Everyone moves to the hidden live line.** If you had chosen the safe or compact strip before, you now start on the thin dark line too, once, with a chat note. If the app says it can't read it (remote desktop, colour filters), type `/bf live safe`. That choice now sticks.

**Community sharing recovers faster.** After failed connection attempts the app now waits at most an hour (it was up to six), and turning sharing on in Settings → Community retries straight away.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once.
