# Backstage Forever 0.39.1 — ask and answer in one window

From Backstage Coding. Designed by Frank Arena.

**One window for your questions.** Click the owl on your minimap (or type `/bf ask`). The Backstage Forever panel opens over the top-right of your game with the cursor already in its question box. Type your question and press Enter, and the answer appears right under it. Ask a follow-up in the same box and it stacks underneath. Press Esc or click ✕ to close it, and your keyboard goes back to WoW.

The small question box that used to open inside the game is gone. WoW can't show answers from the app, so asking in game always meant two windows. Now the whole conversation happens in the panel.

While you're typing in the panel it stays open. Once you click back into the game, it hides by itself after the time you chose in Settings → Game connection. `/bf ask your question` from the chat line still works, and its answer appears in the same panel without taking your keyboard.

**After updating:** type `/reload` in WoW once.
